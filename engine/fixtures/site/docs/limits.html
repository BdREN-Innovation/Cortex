Plan limits

The Free plan allows 3 projects and 1 GB of storage. The Pro plan allows 25 projects and 100 GB of storage. The Enterprise plan has no project limit.

Rate limits are 60 requests per minute on Free, 600 on Pro and 6000 on Enterprise. Exceeding the limit returns HTTP 429.

Storage overages are billed at 10 cents per gigabyte per month.
