## **Research Areas**

 Research activities are organized across major engineering and applied science domains, encouraging interdisciplinary collaboration and innovation.

- **Civil Engineering:** Structural Engineering, Transportation Systems, Geotechnical Engineering, Water Resources, and Environmental Engineering.
- **Mechanical Engineering:** Thermal Engineering, Manufacturing Systems, Materials Engineering, Robotics, and Energy Systems.
- **Electrical & Electronic Engineering:** Power Systems, Renewable Energy, Control Systems, Signal Processing, and Microelectronics.
- **Computer Science & Engineering:** Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, and Computer Networks.
- **Biomedical Engineering:** Biomedical Signal Processing, Medical Devices, Neuroengineering, and Healthcare Technology.
- **Materials & Chemical Engineering:** Nanomaterials, Polymer Engineering, Chemical Process Design, and Sustainable Materials.
- **Energy & Sustainability:** Renewable Energy, Smart Grids, Energy Efficiency, and Climate-Resilient Infrastructure.

 These research areas reflect the university’s mission to produce innovative engineers, impactful research outcomes, and technology-driven solutions for society.
