The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) has once again showcased its excellence in education, research, and innovation. Known for its cutting-edge academic programs and state-of-the-art research facilities, the department is making significant strides in the fields of Power Systems, Renewable Energy, Artificial Intelligence, and Advanced Communication Systems.In a recent development, the department launched new research initiatives focusing on smart grid technology and renewable energy systems, including projects on thin-film solar cells and energy-efficient power systems. These initiatives are aimed at addressing global energy challenges and fostering sustainable development.

Additionally, the department hosted a series of workshops and seminars featuring distinguished speakers from academia and industry. The IEEE Membership Drive 2025 was a highlight, where students and faculty were encouraged to connect with the global community of technology enthusiasts and professionals.The department also celebrated its students’ remarkable achievements in national and international competitions, including robotics, programming, and circuit design contests. Their innovative projects continue to receive accolades, putting CUET EEE on the map as a hub for future leaders in technology. With a strong commitment to excellence in teaching and research, the CUET EEE Department remains a beacon of innovation, empowering students to contribute to the technological advancements of tomorrow.
