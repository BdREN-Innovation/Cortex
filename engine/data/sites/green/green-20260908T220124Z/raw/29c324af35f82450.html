Message from the Director

Dear Students, Faculty, Colleagues, and Partners,

Welcome to the Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) at Green University of Bangladesh.

In today's rapidly evolving global landscape, the pursuit of excellence in higher education is not merely an objective but a fundamental responsibility. At GUB, we are committed to fulfilling this responsibility by embedding a robust culture of quality, accountability, and continuous improvement into every facet of our institution.

The IQAC serves as the strategic nucleus of this endeavor. Our mission is to work collaboratively with all academic and administrative units to develop, implement, and sustain systems that ensure our programs are relevant, rigorous, and recognized. We focus on enhancing the student learning experience, empowering our faculty through professional development, and aligning our curricula with both national aspirations and international benchmarks.

Quality assurance is a shared journey, one that requires the active participation, feedback, and dedication of every member of our university community. I invite you all to engage with the IQAC, to uphold our standards, and to contribute ideas that propel us forward.

Together, let us build an academic environment where excellence is a habit, innovation is encouraged, and every graduate is equipped to lead with knowledge and integrity.

Thank you for your commitment to quality and to the proud legacy of Green University of Bangladesh.

Warm regards,

Prof. Dr. Khawza Iftekhar Uddin Ahmed
Director, Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC)
Green University of Bangladesh

What is the purpose of academic and administrative audits?

Academic and administrative audits help evaluate compliance with quality standards, identify strengths and areas for improvement, ensure proper documentation, and support evidence-based decision-making for continuous institutional development.

How does IQAC support accreditation processes?

IQAC assists departments in preparing accreditation documents, conducting readiness assessments, maintaining required evidence and records, identifying compliance gaps, and coordinating with stakeholders to ensure successful accreditation outcomes.
