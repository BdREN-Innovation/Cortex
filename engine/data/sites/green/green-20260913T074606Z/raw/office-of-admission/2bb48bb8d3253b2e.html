Dear Prospective Students and Guardians,


I am thrilled to extend a warm welcome to you on behalf of Green University of Bangladesh (GUB). As the Head of Admission, I take pride in introducing GUB as a leading private university in Bangladesh, committed to delivering top-tier education, fostering academic excellence, and nurturing well-rounded individuals.


At GUB, our experienced faculty members, comprising over 250 full-time teachers including 35 PhDs, will guide you to delve into cutting-edge knowledge and explore real-world applications through Outcome-Based Education (OBE)


Here's why GUB stands out:


  • Diverse Programs, Dedicated Faculty: Explore our comprehensive range of programs, led by experienced faculty members who are passionate about their fields. They'll not only guide your academic journey but also mentor your personal and professional development.
  • Cutting-Edge Facilities: Immerse yourself in a stimulating environment with state-of-the-art labs, a rich library featuring both physical and digital resources, and a sprawling green campus that fosters a healthy and inspiring atmosphere for learning.
  • Holistic Development: GUB goes beyond academics. Join a vibrant campus life filled with clubs, sports, and cultural events. Gain real-world experience through internships, research opportunities, and collaborations with leading industry partners.
  • Supportive Community: We understand your journey. Our experienced mentors, including senior students, are here to guide and support you every step of the way. Our strong alumni network also offers valuable advice and connections.
  • Affordable Opportunities: Explore our scholarship and financial aid options, ensuring your education remains accessible. We also offer flexible payment plans to fit your needs.

Experience the GUB difference:


  • Our sprawling green campus fosters creativity, collaboration, and a sense of belonging.
  • Free transportation to and from major locations until June 2024.
  • Comfortable hostel facilities for male and female students.
  • Outcome-Based Education (OBE) for practical and industry-relevant learning.
  • High employment rates in diverse sectors, thanks to our industry connections and career guidance services.

Visit our website (green.edu.bd) to learn more about our programs, facilities, and vibrant student life. We encourage you to schedule a campus visit or attend an online information session to experience GUB firsthand.


Join the Green University family and thrive!


Best regards

Services of Green University of Bangladesh Admission Office

The Admission Office at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB) is committed to guiding prospective students through a smooth and hassle-free admission process. We provide essential support, resources, and guidance to help students and their guardians make informed decisions about their academic journey.

Admission Activities

Our Admission Office remains active throughout the year, ensuring an efficient and accessible admission process. We respond to inquiries, provide comprehensive admission support, and facilitate the application process for both undergraduate and postgraduate programs.

Services We Offer

  • ✅ Admission Guidance – Our dedicated team provides one-on-one counseling to help students choose the right academic program based on their career aspirations and eligibility. We assist with understanding admission requirements, prerequisites, and academic pathways.
  • ✅ Application Support – We guide students through the entire application process, ensuring they submit required documents correctly and meet deadlines. Our team also helps resolve any application-related issues to facilitate a smooth admission experience.
  • ✅ Information Sessions & Open Houses – We regularly organize information sessions, webinars, and open house events to provide insights into GUB’s academic programs, campus facilities, and student life. Prospective students and parents can interact with faculty members, current students, and alumni to gain a better understanding of GUB.
  • ✅ Scholarship & Financial Aid Information – GUB offers various scholarships and tuition waivers to deserving students based on merit, financial need, and special categories. Our team provides detailed information about available scholarships, eligibility criteria, and the application process.
  • ✅ Campus Tours & On-Site Assistance – Prospective students and guardians can visit the green and modern campus of GUB to explore state-of-the-art classrooms, laboratories, library, student lounges, and recreational areas. Our team arranges guided campus tours, allowing visitors to experience the university environment firsthand.
  • ✅ Digital & Social Media Support – We actively engage with prospective students through our website, social media pages, and online chat support, ensuring they receive prompt responses to their queries. Our online application portal is designed to provide a user-friendly admission process.
  • ✅ Exam & Admission Test Assistance – Some programs require entrance exams or interviews for admission. We provide guidance on test formats, sample questions, and preparation resources to help applicants succeed.
  • ✅ Pre-Enrollment Assistance – After receiving admission confirmation, our team assists students in completing enrollment, course registration, fee payment, and orientation programs to ensure a seamless transition into university life.
