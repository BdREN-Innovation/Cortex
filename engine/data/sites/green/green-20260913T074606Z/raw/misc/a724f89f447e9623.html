Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our users ask the most.

1. What is the Center for Language and Cultural Studies (CLCS)?

The Center for Language and Cultural Studies (CLCS) at Green University of Bangladesh is dedicated to promoting multilingual education, intercultural understanding, and lifelong learning through language courses, cultural programs, workshops, and academic

2. What language courses does CLCS offer?

CLCS offers a variety of language courses, including Japanese, Chinese, Korean, English, and other languages based on demand and academic planning. New courses are introduced regularly

3. Who can enroll in CLCS language courses?

Our courses are open to Green University of Bangladesh students, faculty members, staff, and, in many cases, external participants who wish to develop their language skills.

4. Do I need prior knowledge of the language?

No. Most courses begin at the beginner level. However, placement or assessment may be conducted for learners seeking admission to higher-level courses.

5. How do I register for a course?

You can register online through the CLCS website or by visiting the CLCS office during office hours. Registration instructions are announced before each course intake.

6. What is the duration of a language course?

Course duration varies depending on the language and proficiency level. Most certificate courses run for several weeks with scheduled classes each week

7. How much is the course fee?

Course fees vary depending on the language and course level. The latest fee structure is available on each course page or through the CLCS office.

8. Will I receive a certificate after completing the course?

Yes. Participants who successfully complete the course and meet the attendance and assessment requirements receive a certificate from CLCS

9. Are there any examinations or assessments?

Yes. Most courses include quizzes, assignments, class participation, and a final assessment to evaluate learners' progress.

10. Does CLCS organize cultural events?

Yes. CLCS regularly organizes cultural festivals, language exchange sessions, seminars, workshops, guest lectures, competitions, and international collaboration programs to enhance learners' cultural awareness.

11. Can I join more than one course at the same time?

Yes, provided that the class schedules do not conflict and you can meet the academic requirements of each course.

12. Where are CLCS classes held?

Classes are generally conducted on the Green University of Bangladesh campus. Some courses or activities may also be offered in hybrid or online formats when applicable.

13. How can I stay updated about upcoming courses and events?

Visit the CLCS website regularly and follow our official social media channels for announcements regarding admissions, events, workshops, and special programs.

14. How can I contact CLCS?

You can contact the CLCS office through the Contact page on our website, email us, or visit our office during regular working hours for any inquiries.
