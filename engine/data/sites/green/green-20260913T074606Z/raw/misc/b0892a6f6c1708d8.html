STI 2026
🎯

Vision & Scope

The Sustainable Technologies for Industry 5.0 (STI 2026) conference invites original research contributions that explore innovative and sustainable solutions aligned with the emerging paradigms of Industry 4.0 and Industry 5.0. As industries evolve to become smarter, more automated, and increasingly human-centric, STI 2026 serves as a premier international platform for researchers, practitioners, and industry professionals to present cutting-edge advancements that drive this transformation.

⚡

Focus Areas

This year's conference will focus on research areas that include but are not limited to cyber-physical systems, smart manufacturing, robotics, intelligent energy systems, sustainable textile innovations, and human-AI collaboration. It places particular emphasis on technologies that address the intersection of automation, sustainability, and human-in-the-loop design, core tenets of Industry 5.0. The conference also aims to foster discussion around the socio-technical and environmental aspects of modern industry.

📍

Venue & Date

STI 2026 will be held on 03-04 December 2026 at the permanent campus of Green University of Bangladesh, located in Purbachal American City, Dhaka.

📚

Publication

Accepted and presented papers (excluding those from the textile engineering domain) will be submitted to the IEEE Xplore Digital Library, subject to IEEE's final approval, and are expected to be indexed in Scopus and other major bibliographic databases. Papers from the textile engineering domain will be included in the official conference proceedings.

🔍

Review Process

All submissions must be original, unpublished work and will undergo a double-blind peer-review process to ensure high academic and ethical standards. We strongly encourage contributions from both academia and industry that showcase real-world applications, prototypes, theoretical developments, and interdisciplinary approaches.

🤝

Join Us

We welcome you to be a part of STI 2026 and contribute to shaping the future of sustainable, intelligent industry. Join us in this global forum to share ideas, discover innovations, and build partnerships that matter.
