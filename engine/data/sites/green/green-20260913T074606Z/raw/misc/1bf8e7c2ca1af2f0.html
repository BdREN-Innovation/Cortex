Message from the Director

I am delighted to be associated with the Center for Career Development (CCD) at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB).

CCD was established in 2013 at GUB, with the vision to develop graduates with skills that make them competent in the job market. Besides, another objective of CCD was not only to provide jobs to students/graduates but also to prepare them for the competitive job market so they can easily secure their cherished jobs through the skills and knowledge they acquire at GUB.

All students in the final two semesters (11 and 12) must enroll in a non-credit course, Professional Life Skill Development (PLSD), conducted by the CCD. The course has been designed to develop students' skills, including CV writing, interview preparation, presentation skills, professional etiquette, and grooming. The experienced industrial experts are invited to conduct the session of this course as guest speakers, and they assess the students with their constructive guidance to help them prepare for future entry into the job market.

CCD is also responsible for organizing seminars, workshops, job fairs, and sessions to support the development of graduates and current students. On the other hand, CCD is working to foster industry collaboration and to identify internship and job placement opportunities continuously. I am very grateful to our alumni body, those who are working in the industry with success, shining in their careers, and reflecting the name and glory of GUB as its mirror. They are also supporting CCD's activities to create more opportunities for current students so they can follow in the footsteps of alumni in the near future.

Meanwhile, I am also happy to see that our graduates are working in different national, international, and multinational corporations, as well as renowned banks, in leading positions at home and abroad. We also support our students and graduates in their career planning and help them choose the right track by evaluating their academic results, extracurricular activities, organizational skills, and, most importantly, leadership qualities.

Lastly, I would like to say that I firmly believe that, as a GUB family member, it is producing skilled, quality graduates for the job market. They can compete in any situation, and prospectively, they will be at the peak of their careers in the very near future. CCD is always with you all and will always be for a better journey together.

I wish all the success and prosperity to the Green University of Bangladesh (GUB) and the Center for Career Development (CCD) as well.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions our users ask the most.

10. Whom should I contact if I have further questions?

You can reach us via email: ccd.office@green.edu.bd , call: 01324713519 , or visit our office: Room No 208, 1st floor, Admin Building , Green University of Bangladesh

9. Is there any fee for using your services?

No, all Center for Career Development services are free for registered students and alumni.

8. How can I stay updated about new job or internship opportunities?

Follow our social media pages, and also regularly check the vacancy page, notice section on our website for the latest postings.

7. Does the Center organize campus recruitment drives?

Yes, we collaborate with companies to host campus recruitment drives and career fairs throughout the academic year.

6. Can you help me improve my resume or cover letter?

Yes, we are offering personalized reviews and feedback on your resume and cover letters to help you present your best self to employers. Our PLSD course also help you to build an industry driven CV with the help of PLSD faculties.

5. Are there any workshops or seminars for career development?

Absolutely! We regularly organize workshops and seminars on topics such as resume writing, interview skills, networking, and industry trends. Check our events, notice and news pages for upcoming sessions.

4. Does the Center help with internship placements?

Yes, we assist students in finding suitable internships by sharing opportunities and connecting students with industry partners.

3. How can I schedule a career counseling appointment?

You can schedule an appointment by visiting our office during working hours, calling at our official number, or booking online through our website’s appointment portal. Please check this: https://ccd.green.edu.bd/pages/book-an-appointment

2. Who can use the Center for Career Development services?

All current students and alumni of the university/institution are welcome to use our services.

1. What services does the Center for Career Development offer?

The Center provides career counseling, resume and cover letter reviews, job search assistance, interview preparation, internship and job placement support, skill development workshops, and networking opportunities.
