Application for Provisional Certificate and Official Transcript

** Students who have completed all the required courses of the curriculum are eligible to apply. Issuance of Certificate or Transcript requires approval/clearance from several authorities. After final approval by the respective Department Chairperson, the Controller of Examinations office requires an additional Seven (7) working days to complete the processing. Students are advised to apply well in advance of the required date. You are requested to go through the user manual before submitting the application. If necessary, call-01324713515.
