Celebrating Excellence

5th Convocation of GUB

The Green University of Bangladesh proudly announces the commencement of its 5th Convocation, a momentous occasion that marks the culmination of years of dedication, hard work, and intellectual pursuit. Set against the backdrop of academic excellence and a commitment to sustainable education, the event promises to be a celebration of achievements, dreams, and the limitless potential of our graduates.

Previous Convocations

Fourth Convocation 2021

The 4th Convocation of GUB was held on June 19, 2021, in virtual space using Zoom. Honorable Education Minister Dr. Dipu Moni, MP chaired the ceremony as the representative of His Excellence the President of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh Mr. Md. Abdul Hamid, the Chancellor of the university and conferred degrees on the graduates.

Third Convocation 2018

The 3rd Convocation of Green University of Bangladesh was held on January 17, 2018 at the Permanent Campus of the university, Purbachal American City. Education Minister Nurul Islam Nahid, MP was the key man of the convocation ceremony as the representative of the President and Chancellor of the university and conferred degrees on the graduates

Second Convocation 2016

The 2nd Convocation of Green University of Bangladesh was held on February 18, 2016 at International Convention City Bashundhara. Mr. Nurul Islam Nahid, MP, the hon’ble Education Minister, People’s Republic of Bangladesh presided over the convocation ceremony as the representative of the hon’ble President and Chancellor of the University

First Convocation 2012

GUB held its First Convocation, a solemn and sacred graduation ceremony, on 16 February 2012 at Bangabandhu International Conference Center (BICC) to award degrees to more than four hundred graduates. The Honorable Education Minister, Mr. Nurul Islam Nahid, on behalf of the Honorable Chancellor and the President of the People’s Republic of Bangladesh, presided over the Convocation ceremony
