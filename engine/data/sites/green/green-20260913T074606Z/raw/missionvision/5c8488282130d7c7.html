Library Mission and Vision

Mission:

The mission of GUB Library is to facilitate the process of advancing academic excellence and foster inclusion and innovation in the teaching, learning, and research work of our diverse community by providing essential scholarly resources and services to the users.

Vision:

The GUB Library will have continued contribution and influence in the University’s advancement through equitable access to services, inclusive spaces, and unique collections.
