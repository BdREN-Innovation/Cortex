About The Event

ITD 2021 conference is an international initiative of Green University of Bangladesh (GUB) to expedite the spirit of innovation and transformation for development through promoting authentic intellectual works by researchers, academicians and practitioners around the globe in diverse fields of business, law, arts and social sciences to be held on October 23-24, 2021 through Virtual Platform (Zoom).

The conference has been initiated at a time when the world is shaken by Covid 19 pandemic and is facing unforeseen challenges in coping with the situations. The scholars, academicians and scholars are also on the constant look out for avenues and innovations in their respective fields. Hence, research works have garnered remarkable turns, and consequently desired platforms are needed to orchestrate attempts and achievements. In this backdrop, GUB has initiated ITD 2021 to facilitate collaboration and convergence of the researchers of diverse fields for further innovation and development in multidisciplinary mode.

To accomplish this mission, we are cordially seeking original and innovative papers from authors of respective fields. All submissions will be blind peer reviewed and, thereafter presented quality papers will be published in peer reviewed journals of GUB. In addition, the best paper in each track will receive a special award and author(s) of the concerned paper(s) will receive the special grant of 'Free Registration' in the next ITD conference. IDT 2021 welcomes authors around the globe to participate participate in the conference covering the themes Transforming Business through Entrepreneurial Innovation, Development of Human Rights and Legal Affairs in 21st Century and Transformation in Media, Society, Language and Culture.

Submission Guidelines

Submitted manuscript must be a new and original work. The work must not be currently under consideration by any other publication. Each contribution should be sent as e-mail with attachment directly to itd@green.edu.bd both in MS Word and PDF format. In the e-mail, please include author’s name(s), cell number(s) and e-mail address(s), complete postal mail address of the corresponding author, title of the research paper and affiliation(s). A paper identification number will be assigned to each submission for future correspondences and will be e-mailed to the corresponding author.

Email & File Naming Instructions:

  • Please name the attachment file each time you email as per the following format-
    • For Abstract:
      • (Number of Thematic Area)_(Subtheme Number)_abstract_Paper title
      • Example: 2_2.1_abstract_challanges of tax law in BD
    • For Full Paper:
      • (Number of Thematic Area)_(Subtheme Number)_full paper_Paper title
      • Example: 2_2.1_full paper_challanges of tax law in BD
    • For Power Point Presentation:
      • (Number of Thematic Area)_(Subtheme Number)_ppt_Paper title
      • Example: 2_2.1_ppt_challanges of tax law in BD
  • Email to: itd@green.edu.bd
  • Subject line: Abstract Submission/Full Paper Submission

Abstract Submission:

Authors are expected to submit their abstract in both MS Word & PDF format based on the following criteria-

  • Contents: Title, Author’s Name & Affiliation, Abstract & Keywords
  • Abstract length: 150-250 words
  • Font:
    • Title: Times New Roman 15 Point (Bold, No italic & Center alignment)
    • Author’s Name(s): Times New Roman 14Point (No italic & Center alignment)
    • Affiliation: Times New Roman 13 Point (Italic & Center alignment)
    • Abstract & Keywords Heading: Times New Roman 13 Point (Bold, No italic)
    • Abstract body & Keywords: Times New Roman 12 Point (No italic)
  • Line Space: Single
  • Abstract Alignment: Justified
  • Abstract Paragraph: Single & No Indentation
  • Keywords: Maximum 6, in alphabetical order separated with semicolons and not included in the title.

Full Paper Submission:

After the acceptance of the abstract, authors must submit their manuscript following the instructions bellow-

  • Writing must be submitted in single-column format using double line-spacing, and 12 point Times New Roman front in A4 size paper with 1 inch margin each side.
  • Manuscripts must not exceed 25 pages on Custom size paper including everything (paper title, abstract, keywords, body texts, such as- introduction, methods, equations, results and discussions, conclusions, figures, tables, acknowledgement, references, appendices etc.).
  • All manuscripts will undergo plagiarism/similarity check software and the maximum acceptable limit of acknowledged similarity is 25% (excluding the reference section).
  • Abstract and keywords must not exceed 250 and 6 words respectively.
  • The paper length should be around 2,500-7,000 words (without reference section)
  • ITD 2021 discourages any form of notes (footnote or endnote)
  • In-text citation must be given in the following format (APA 6th Edition):
    • For single author: (Author’s last name, Year)
    • For two authors: (1st Author’s last name & 2nd Author’s last name, Year)
    • For more than two authors: (1st Author’s last name et al., Year)
  • A separate reference of works must also be given at the last page of the work according to the formats as following (APA 6th Edition):
    • Book: King, M. (2000). Wrestling with the angel: A life of Janet Frame. Aukland, New Zealand: Viking.
    • Book Chapter: Kestly, T. (2010). Group sandplay in elementary schools. In A. A. Drewes & C. E. Shaefer (Eds.), School-based play therapy (2nd ed., pp. 257-282). Hoboken, NJ: John Wileys & Sons.
    • Journal with DOI: Germann, F., Ebbes, P., & Grewal, R. (2015). The chief marketing officer matters! Journal of Marketing, 79(3), 1-22. http://dx.doi.org/10.1509/jm.14.0244
    • Journal with no DOI: Germann, F., Ebbes, P., & Grewal, R. (2015). The chief marketing officer matters! Journal of Marketing, 79(3), 1-22.
    • Book or report by corporate author e.g. organization, association, government department: International Labour Organization. (2007). Equality at work: Tackling the challenges (International Labour Conference report). Geneva, Switzerland: Author.
    • Conference paper online: Bochner, S. (1996, November). Mentoring in higher education: Issues to be addressed in developing a mentoring program. Paper presented at the Australian Association for Research in Education Conference, Singapore. Retrieved from http://www.aare.edu.au/96pap/bochs96018.txt
    • Film: Preston, G. (Director/Producer). (2010). Home by Christmas [Motion picture]. New Zealand: Gaylene Preston Production.
    • Magazine or newspaper article: Report casts shadow on biofuel crops. (2007, October 16). Waikato Times, p. 21.
    • Theses: Liu, G. (2014). Improving corporate Internet reporting in China (Doctoral thesis, University of Waikato, Hamilton, New Zealand). Retrieved from http://researchcommons.waikato.ac.nz/handle/10289/2241
    • Webpage: New Zealand Trade and Enterprise. (n.d.). Agribusiness. Retrieved from https://www.nzte.govt.nz/en/export/market-research/agribusiness/

Please be noted that all the submissions, after initial screening by the editorial board, will be sent for blind reviewing, that is, the identities of the authors will be withheld from the reviewers. So, please make sure that the paper title, author's name(s) and affiliation(s) to be appeared in the cover page only. The abstract and the main body should be started from 2nd page having the paper title again. The referencing style may require to be changed as per the requirement of the concerned journal.

Authors must keep in mind that final paper acceptance will be considered as valid till the last date of registration and failing to complete the registration process on time may void the probable participation in the real conference event.

Authors are advised to upload full papers through Easy Chair conference management system using this link https://easychair.org/conferences/?conf=itd2021

For further information or in case of emergency, please send e-mail to: itd@green.edu.bd
