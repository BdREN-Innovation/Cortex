Office of the Exam Controller

  1. Administration
  2. Office of the Exam Controller
 

Dr. Md. Ahsanul Alam

Controller of Examinations

Welcome to the Office of the Controller of Examinations at Green University of Bangladesh (GUB).

The Office of the Controller of Examinations is dedicated to ensuring the integrity, transparency, and efficiency of the University’s academic assessment and record-management systems. Our primary responsibility is to uphold the highest standards in examination administration, result processing, certification, and academic record maintenance while supporting the University’s commitment to academic excellence.

At GUB, we recognize that assessment plays a crucial role in the learning process. Working closely with academic departments and faculty members, we strive to ensure that examinations and evaluation procedures are conducted fairly, accurately, and in accordance with established academic policies and quality assurance standards.

Our office is committed to providing timely and reliable services to students, graduates, faculty members, and other stakeholders. Through continuous improvement and the effective use of technology, we seek to enhance the efficiency, security, and accessibility of examination-related services while maintaining the confidentiality and accuracy of academic records.

We also aim to provide responsive support and clear guidance regarding examination procedures, academic regulations, transcripts, certificates, and credential verification. As our graduates pursue higher education and professional opportunities both nationally and internationally, we remain committed to facilitating reliable and efficient academic documentation services.

The credibility of a university depends significantly on the quality and trustworthiness of its assessment processes. Therefore, we place strong emphasis on accountability, professionalism, and service excellence in all our activities.

I sincerely thank our students, faculty members, officers, and staff for their continued cooperation and support. Together, we will continue to uphold the academic standards and reputation of Green University of Bangladesh and contribute to the development of skilled, ethical, and globally competent graduates.

Dr. Md. Ahsanul Alam
