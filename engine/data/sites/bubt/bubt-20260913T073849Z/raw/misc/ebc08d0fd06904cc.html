Available Payment Methods

bKash

Pay directly using the bKash App.

Transaction Charge: 1.15% of the total amount.

Recommended for fast, mobile-friendly payments.

Rocket / Nexus Pay

Make payments using the Rocket or Nexus Pay App.

Transaction Charge: Maximum Tk. 50 (regardless of amount).

Best for large payments due to fixed max charge.

SSLCommerz

Use multiple methods like cards (Visa/MasterCard), bKash, Rocket, and more through the SSLCommerz Gateway.

Transaction Charge: 1.4% of the total amount.

Flexible and supports multiple gateways in one place.
