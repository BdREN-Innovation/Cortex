      2. Time Period Guaranteed Response Time
      6 am to 9 pm 2.5 hours
      9pm to 6 am 6 hours
    2. For Computing Services:
      3. Time Period Guaranteed Response Time
      6 am to 12 am 1 hour
      12am to 6 am 4 hour
