Research Coordinators

Home > About > Research Coordinators

The Research Coordinator plays a vital role in leading and managing the research activities within the department. The primary responsibility is to ensure the effective development, implementation, and coordination of research initiatives that align with the department's academic goals and the university’s broader research strategy. They are playing a vital role for the department to expand its research portfolio and enhance its impact both locally and globally.
