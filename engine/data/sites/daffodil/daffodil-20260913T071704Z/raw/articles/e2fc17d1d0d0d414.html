IamDaffodilian

December 22, 2022 · Main

Daffodil International University Alumni Association (DIUAA) is delighted to introduce the "I Am Daffodilian" Smart Card an advanced chip-based card designed exclusively for our alumni. This card offers a range of benefits and privileges, making life easier and more rewarding. We are happy to present you with the benefits of our alumni card-

  • It carries a unique identity for DIU alumni
  • Seamless Campus Access
  • Parking Facility
  • DIU alumni can get exclusive discounts and offers at restaurants, educational institutes, and various services.
