About DIU Alumni Association (DIUAA)

December 22, 2022 · Main

The Daffodil International University Alumni Association (DIUAA) was established on September 7, 2008, with the primary goal of strengthening and sustaining the bond between the university and its ever-growing network of alumni. Under the theme "Connecting Alumni, Inspiring Growth," DIUAA fosters a sense of community and solidarity, bringing together graduates from diverse fields and backgrounds to collaborate, share knowledge, and support each other personally and professionally.

DIUAA serves as a vital platform for alumni to actively engage, collaborate, and contribute to the university's continued growth and success. The association's objectives include promoting professional development, providing networking opportunities, and creating a supportive community that offers resources for career advancement. DIUAA also plays a crucial role in mentoring current students, helping them transition from university life to their professional careers through mentorship programs and other initiatives.

The association offers a wide range of benefits to its members, including exclusive access to networking events, professional development opportunities, and lifelong learning resources. By advocating for the interests of DIU graduates, DIUAA enhances the reputation and global presence of the university. Members also have the opportunity to directly contribute to the growth and development of both DIU and the alumni network.

DIUAA is committed to operating transparently, inclusively, and in collaboration with alumni, students, and the university. This collaborative approach helps build long-term relationships that not only benefit the university but also strengthen the global alumni network.
