Career Resources

March 10, 2025 · Main

"Wherever you are in your career journey, Daffodil International University Alumni Association (DIUAA) is committed to connecting you with the resources you need"

 

Career Guidance-

At DIUAA, in alliance with Skill Jobs, Career Development Center (CDC) and Human Resource Development Institute (HRDI) we are dedicated to providing exceptional career development support. Our comprehensive resources are designed to help you navigate your career path and achieve your professional goals. Explore the following resources to enhance your career prospects:-

  • Training
  • Employability 360
  • Employability Assessment
  • Career Guideline Tips from CDC
  • On Campus Job Recruitment
