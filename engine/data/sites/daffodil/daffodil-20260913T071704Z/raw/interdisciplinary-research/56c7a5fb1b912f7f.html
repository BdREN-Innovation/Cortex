Physical Facilities of DIU for Interdisciplinary Research Teams

At Daffodil International University (DIU), research is the cornerstone of our academic excellence and a driving force for innovation and impact. The Division of Research (DoR) mission is to cultivate a dynamic and sustainable research culture that empowers our faculty and students to contribute meaningfully to the global body of knowledge. It is dedicated to establishing a research ecosystem that is smooth, effective, and well-coordinated, ensuring that every researcher has the necessary support to excel.
