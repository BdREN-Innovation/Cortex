About Us

Empowering DIU Through Technology

The IT Section of Daffodil International University is dedicated to building a secure, efficient, and innovation-driven digital environment. Our mission is to support teaching, learning, research, and administration through modern technology infrastructure and responsive IT services.

We continuously work to integrate advanced IT solutions across all university operations — ensuring reliability, accessibility, and data security for every member of the DIU community. By combining innovation with service excellence, DIU IT empowers the university to move toward a smarter, more connected, and technology-driven future.

DIU IT ACADEMY

DIU IT Academy is our dedicated center for IT skill development, offering workshops, training, and certifications in areas like network administration, cloud computing, and software development — empowering future tech leaders within the university.
