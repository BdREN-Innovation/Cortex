What documents are required for application?

  1. Online Application is Mandatory: 
     
  2. For Local Students:
    1. SSC/ Dakhil/ O-Level/ Diploma Certificate & Marksheet
  3. 2. HSC/ Alim/ A-Level/ Diploma Certificate & Marksheet (Diploma 1-8 Marksheets)
  4. 3. Bachelor's Certificate & Marksheet for Master's program
  5. 4. NID of student, father, mother (spouse if any). If the student’s NID is not available, a Birth Certificate can be accepted temporarily. However, the NID must be submitted later.

  7. 6. Certificates & Marksheets should be Education Board copy (Online copy is not accepted)
  8. 7. Bachelor's Certificate & Marksheet should be attested by the respective university Registrar
  9. 8. For quota-based waiver application, relevant certificates/documents have to be submitted
    9. Experience letter, if any, for Master's program admission

  10. Required documents for Issuing the Offer Letter for Admission:

Is it possible to apply before the HSC/ equivalent result is published?

  1. a) No. 
  2. b) After the result publication, students can admit submitting the published online result copy with condition.

Do you allow year-gap students for any program?

  • For Bachelor’s Programs: 
  • We accept a maximum of a 2-year gap for any Bachelor’s program. Applicants with longer gaps are advised to contact the admission office for further guidance.
  •  
  • For Master’s Programs: 
  • There is no gap limitation after completing a Bachelor’s program for admission into Master’s programs.

Do I need to take an entrance exam?

  1. a) Admission at DIU follows a competitive, holistic, and transparent process. It considers the applicant's academic achievements, potential for academic success, and personal characteristics, promoting diversity.
  2.  
  3. b) DIU is an open-access university. Admission can be obtained through an admission tests, which are standard procedures. Prospective students may apply with certified copies of their prior credentials and/or experience. 

Do you accept credit transfers?

  • a) Yes, we do. 
  • b) Students wishing to transfer from another university must have the official transcripts of courses and grades, together with copies of certificate/ mark sheets of SSC or HSC or transcripts of O and A levels. These transcripts will be evaluated against the minimum entry requirement at DIU.
  • c) Also, an NOC has to be submitted from the previous university.

What is the course registration?

  • Before the semester begins, after the payment of required fees to the Accounts Section and getting the clearance from the Accounts Section, students must select their courses under the guidance of their department. This process is known as course registration.

How can I change the Department/Program?

  • Students who wish to change their department or program should follow these steps:
  •  
  • Newly Admitted Student ( First Semester Students) :
  • After immediate admission, if the student wants to change the program, contact admission office before course registration.

 

  1. Running Students:
  • Collect the Change Form: Obtain the Department/Program Change Form from the Admission Office.
  • Fill Out the Form: Complete the form and get the necessary signatures from the respective Dean of the Faculty and Head of the Department.
  • Clear All Dues: Ensure all dues are cleared and the payment of the program change fee. And then collect the sign from the Account section.
  • Submit for Approval: Submit the completed form to the Registrar’s Office for approval.
