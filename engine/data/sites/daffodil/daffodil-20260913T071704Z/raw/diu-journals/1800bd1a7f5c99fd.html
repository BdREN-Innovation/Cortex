DIU Journal of Science and Technology (DIUJST)

Daffodil International University Journal of Science and Technology (DIUJST) is a peer-reviewed annual journal by DIU’s Faculty of Science and Information Technology. It offers a platform for academics, researchers, and industry professionals to share innovative research, especially with practical or industrial applications. DIUJST accepts original work, including extended conference papers, and is published both in print and online each July.

DIU Journal of Business & Entrepreneurship

The DIU Journal of Business and Entrepreneurship (DIUJBE) is a high standard double-blind peer-reviewed research journal published by Daffodil International University. It is affiliated to the Faculty of Business and Entrepreneurship.

DIU Journal of Humanities & Social Sciences

The DIU Journal of Humanities and Social Science (DIUJHSS) is a high standard double-blind peer-reviewed research journal published by Daffodil International University. It is affiliated to the Faculty of Business and Entrepreneurship.

Journal of Emerging Global Health (JEGH)

The Journal of Emerging Global Health (JEGH) is a well-established, peer-reviewed journal published by Daffodil International University and affiliated with the Faculty of Health and Life Sciences (Formerly known as DIU Journal of Allied Health Sciences). The journal's first issue was released in January 2014, initially published twice a year. Over time, JEGH expanded its publication frequency, releasing three issues per year until it transitioned into a quarterly publication in 2025 to accommodate the increasing volume of high-quality research submissions.
