List of Selected Candidates – 1st Admission Test for Fall 2026 Trimester
Publish Date : August 24, 2026

BBA, BBA in AIS, BSECO, BSCSE, BSDS, BSEEE, BSCE, BSSEDS, BSSMSJ, BA in English and BSBGE Programs

 

The list of candidates, who have qualified in the 1st Admission Test for Fall 2026 Trimester, held on August 21, 2026 (Friday), is given under the terms and conditions for admissions mentioned below:

 

Step 1: Selected candidates are advised to log in to https://admission.uiu.ac.bd using the username and password provided at the time of Admission Form submission. Upon logging in, candidates must complete the submission of all remaining required information.

Step 2: Online Admission or on-campus Admission

Selected candidates will be able to take admission from their homes via online; alternatively, they can visit the UIU Office of Admissions within September 9, 2026 (Wednesday). 

 

Admission Procedure – Online

Selected candidates, who will take admission via online, are required to send following information and scanned copies of attested photocopies of their academic and other documents to the email address – admissions@uiu.ac.bd:

Information to be mentioned in the email: Name of the Selected Candidate, Admission Test ID, Payment ID (Admission form), Mobile number; and the name of the desired program for admission.

List of documents to be attached in the email: Certificates, Mark sheets/Transcript and Testimonials for all Examinations, student’s National ID card/Birth registration Certificate/Passport copy, National ID card of the student’s father/mother.

After the initial verification of the documents, the UIU Office of Admissions will notify you to pay the admission fee.

After receiving notification from the Admissions Office, the candidate must log in to https://admission.uiu.ac.bd using the username and password provided during the submission of the Admission Form. Once logged in, the “Pay Admission Fee” button will be visible on the homepage. The candidate should then pay the total Admission Fee of Tk. 22,000 ( Tk. 20,000 as admission fee and Tk. 2,000 as refundable caution money for ID card) using any of the following payment methods: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, DBBL Nexus), Mobile Banking services (Bkash, Rocket, UPay, SureCash, t-Cash, etc.), or Internet Banking (AB Bank, City Bank, iPay, etc.). Please note that a service charge will apply for online payments.

Please note that discrepancy in any document of a student may lead to cancellation of admission of the concerned student at any time.

Candidates, who will take admission via online, would also require submitting the attested photocopies of above-mentioned documents before classes begin.

 

Admission Procedure – On campus

Selected candidates can take admission at UIU Office of Admissions after paying requisite Admission Fee (Tk. 20,000) and Caution Money for ID Card (Tk. 2,000 – refundable) in cash at UIU Finance and Accounts Office on the working days (Saturday to Wednesday) during 9.00 AM to 3.00 PM (Except Govt. Holidays).

Candidates, who will take admission at UIU Campus, must submit the attested photocopies of all Certificates, Testimonials and Transcripts/Mark Sheets, National ID Card/Birth Registration Certificate/Copy of Passport of the candidate, National ID Card of Father/Mother, 2 (two) copies of passport-sized photograph and 1 (one) copy of stamp-sized photograph (which is uploaded online) to the UIU Admissions Office at the time of taking admission. Please note that discrepancy in any document of a student may lead to cancellation of admission of the concerned student at any time.

The list of candidates, who have qualified in the 1st Admission Test for Fall 2026 Trimester, held on August 21, 2026 (Friday), is given under the terms and conditions for admissions mentioned below:

 

Step 1: Selected candidates are advised to log in to https://admission.uiu.ac.bd using the username and password provided at the time of Admission Form submission. Upon logging in, candidates must complete the submission of all remaining required information.

Step 2: Online Admission or on-campus Admission

Selected candidates will be able to take admission from their homes via online; alternatively, they can visit the UIU Office of Admissions within September 9, 2026 (Wednesday). 

 

Admission Procedure – Online

Selected candidates, who will take admission via online, are required to send following information and scanned copies of attested photocopies of their academic and other documents to the email address – admissions@uiu.ac.bd:

Information to be mentioned in the email: Name of the Selected Candidate, Admission Test ID, Payment ID (Admission form), Mobile number; and the name of the desired program for admission.

List of documents to be attached in the email: Certificates, Mark sheets/Transcript and Testimonials for all Examinations, student’s National ID card/Birth registration Certificate/Passport copy, National ID card of the student’s father/mother. EMBA program applicants need to provide a two-year job experience certificate.

 

After the initial verification of the documents, the UIU Office of Admissions will notify you to pay the admission fee.

After receiving notification from the Admissions Office, the candidate must log in to https://admission.uiu.ac.bd using the username and password provided during the submission of the Admission Form. Once logged in, the “Pay Admission Fee” button will be visible on the homepage. The candidate should then pay the total Admission Fee of Tk. 22,000 ( Tk. 20,000 as admission fee and Tk. 2,000 as refundable caution money for ID card) using any of the following payment methods: Credit/Debit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex, DBBL Nexus), Mobile Banking services (Bkash, Rocket, UPay, SureCash, t-Cash, etc.), or Internet Banking (AB Bank, City Bank, iPay, etc.). Please note that a service charge will apply for online payments.

Please note that discrepancy in any document of a student may lead to cancellation of admission of the concerned student at any time.

Candidates, who will take admission via online, would also require submitting the attested photocopies of above-mentioned documents before classes begin.

 

Admission Procedure – On campus

Selected candidates can take admission at UIU Office of Admissions after paying requisite Admission Fee (Tk. 20,000) and Caution Money for ID Card (Tk. 2,000 – refundable) in cash at UIU Finance and Accounts Office on the working days (Saturday to Wednesday) during 9.00 AM to 3.00 PM (Except Govt. Holidays).

Candidates, who will take admission at UIU Campus, must submit the attested photocopies of all Certificates, Testimonials and Transcripts/Mark Sheets, National ID Card/Birth Registration Certificate/Copy of Passport of the candidate, National ID Card of Father/Mother, 2 (two) copies of passport-sized photograph and 1 (one) copy of stamp-sized photograph (which is uploaded online) to the UIU Admissions Office at the time of taking admission. Please note that discrepancy in any document of a student may lead to cancellation of admission of the concerned student at any time.
