Biography

Dr. Md. Motaharul Islam joined United International University (UIU) as a Full Professor, in the Dept. of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) in January 2020. As the administrative responsibility, he served as the Director of the Master of  Computer Science and Engineering Program at UIU.  Before joining UIU, he had been serving as an Associate Professor in the CSE Department of BRAC University. He had also served as an Assistant Professor in the Dept. of Computer Science at Islamic University of Madinah (IUM), Saudi Arabia. Before traveling to Saudi Arabia, he had been working as an Assistant Professor in the CSE Department of Islamic University of Technology (IUT), OIC, Gazipur, Bangladesh. He has been awarded PhD in Computer Engineering from Kyung Hee University, South Korea in the field of Smart Internet of Things in 2013. Dr. Motahar had also served as the CTO of BERNET and the founding CTO of BdREN of University Grants Commission of Bangladesh. As community service, he has been working as a Director (Communications) for the Bangladesh Local Chapter of International Network for Outcome based Education (IN4OBE) founded by Prof. Dr. William Spady. He has also been serving as the editorial board member and reviewers of different international journals. He has published around 120 research articles in reputed international journals and conferences. His research interests include Smart IoT, NB-IoT, IP-WSN, Cloud Computing, Network Virtualization, Edge Intelligence, Machine Learning etc. For the last 10 years, Dr. Motahar had been actively engaged in outcome based education (OBE) for ABET and BAETE. He led the OBE Committee, Curriculum Committee at UIU and BRACU. He also served as the adviser of IEEE CS students chapters in different universities.
