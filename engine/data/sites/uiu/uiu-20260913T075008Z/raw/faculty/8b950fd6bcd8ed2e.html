Biography

Prof. Dr. A.K.M. Muzahidul Islam was born in Shariatpur district of Bangladesh. He has received M.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering with Honours from Kharkiv National University of Radio Electronics (NURE), Ukraine, in 1999 and D.Eng. in the field of Computer Science and Engineering from Nagoya Institute of Technology (NiTech), Japan in 2007. He has received Japanese Government Monbusho Scholarship (October 2002 – March 2006). Prof. Muzahid has over Six (06) years of Industrial experiences in Bangladesh and in Japan. In January 2011, Prof. Muzahid has started his career as an Academician. Until January 2017, he served as a Senior Lecturer at Malaysia-Japan International Institute of Technology (MJIIT) of Universiti Teknologi Malaysia (UTM), Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. He has served as the IT Manager and a Member of the Management Committee of MJIIT for around 05 years. He then served as an Associate Professor and Head of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at the University of Liberal Arts Bangladesh (ULAB), Bangladesh until September 2018. Currently, he is a Professor in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) at United International University (UIU), Bangladesh. Earlier, he has served as the Director of the Directorate of Career Counselling and Student Affairs (DCCSA) and  the Director of Center for International Affairs and Cooperation (CIAC). His research interests include Network Architecture, Communication Protocol, Cognitive Radio Network, Software Defined Radio, Wireless Sensor Network, IoT, Cloud Computing, Smart Healthcare, Smart Agriculture, etc., whereas his teaching interests include Structured Programming Language, Computer Networks, Data Communication, Wireless Sensor Network, Cloud Computing, Internet of Things (IoT), etc. Prof. Muzahid has published 128 Research Articles (including 01 Book, 02 Book Chapter, 51 peer-reviewed Journals and 74 Conference Papers) and 2,450+ Google Citations with H-Index of 25 and 1515+ Scopus Citation with H-Index of 19 and 118 Publications. He has secured more than 10 National and International Research Grants. Prof. Muzahid has supervised 13 PhD students (05 – as the Main Supervisor and 08 – as a Co-Supervisor), 10 master’s students, and over 50 BEng Final Year Project through their graduation. Prof. Muzahid is the initiator and General Chair of ICBBDB 2021 (Technically Co-Sponsored by CCIS, Springer), and WICBBDB 2023 & WICBBDB 2024 Workshops. He is also the initiator and Technical Program Chair in ICAICT 2016 and ICAICT 2020 Int’l Conferences (ICAICT 2020 Technically Co-Sponsored by IEEE Bangladesh Section). He has served as the TPC Chair in the ETCCE 2020 and ETCCE 2021 Int’l Conferences and as Secretariat in the ICaTAS 2016 Int’l Conference, Malaysia and 7TH AUN/SEED-Net 2014 Int’l Conference on EEE. He was an Advisory Member of ICBAPS2015, Malaysia, and also played as the Communication Track Chair at ICIEV15, ICAEE 2015, ICAEE 2017 and ICAEE 2019 Int’l Conferences. Dr. Muzahid has served as an Associate Editor in the International Journal of Computer and Information Technology (IJCIT).
