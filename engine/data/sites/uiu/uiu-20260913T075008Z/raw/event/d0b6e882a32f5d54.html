The UIU Social Services Club, one of the leading clubs under the Directorate of Career Counseling and Student Affairs (DCCSA) of United International University (UIU), is going to organize a two-day “Blood Donation Campaign 2026” along with a “Thalassemia Awareness Seminar” on 5–6 September 2026 (Saturday and Sunday), in association with Bangladesh Thalassemia Samity & Hospital (BTS), a non-profit voluntary organization dedicated to serving Thalassemia patients and promoting awareness and prevention of Thalassemia.

Thalassemia Awareness Seminar

In Bangladesh, Thalassemia is a significant public health concern. A person can be completely healthy and show no noticeable symptoms while still being a Thalassemia carrier. According to the National Thalassemia Survey 2024, approximately 11.4% of people aged 14–35 are Thalassemia carriers, meaning that about 1 in every 9 young people is a Thalassemia carrier. To raise awareness about Thalassemia, its prevention, carrier screening, and the importance of early detection, a “Thalassemia Awareness Seminar” will be held on 5 September 2026 (Saturday).

Blood Donation Campaign

Thalassemia is a genetic blood disorder in which many patients depend on regular blood transfusions for survival. Your few minutes can become someone else’s second chance at life. As part of the campaign, blood donors will receive the following complimentary screening reports and benefits:

  • Blood Grouping
  • HBsAg — Hepatitis B
  • Anti-HCV — Hepatitis C
  • Anti-HIV
  • Malaria
  • Syphilis
  • Free Mini Health Check-up
  • Donor’s Card
  • Gift (Key Ring)
  • Refreshments
