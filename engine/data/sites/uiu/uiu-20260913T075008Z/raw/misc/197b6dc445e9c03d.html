Admission Requirements

Applicants will be required to sit for an Admission Test designed to judge their abilities and aptitude for the program. The Test will be held as arranged by the University. The Admission Test will be held on the following three areas:

  • 1. Language and Communication

  • 2. Biology

  • 3. Chemistry

Degree Requirements

The degree requirements of B.Sc. in Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering will be as follows:

  • 1. Completion of 140.0 credit hours.

  • 2. Passing of all courses individually and maintaining a minimum CGPA of 2.00.

Overview of The Department of Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering.

UIU is going to expand its academic arena in the field of biomedical research and healthcare industries. The establishment of “The Department of Biotechnology and Genetic Engineering” as a new department under the School of Life Sciences will be an excellent addition to contribute in biomedical research and education.
