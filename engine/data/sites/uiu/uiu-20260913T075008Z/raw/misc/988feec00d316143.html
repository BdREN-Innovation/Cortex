Welcome to The Department of Media Studies and Journalism

The Department of Media Studies and Journalism (MSJ) at United International University (UIU) offers a four-year undergraduate program covering the diverse fields of human communication, media studies, and journalism. After completing BSS (Honours) in Media Studies and Journalism (MSJ) Program, the students will become suitable professionals by learning theoretical perspectives, practical know-hows, professional ethics, and human values.

Program offered by the Department

The Department of Media Studies and Journalism program is meticulously designed with the goal of becoming a prominent institution for media education, research, and professional practice. Our graduates are poised to take on leadership roles in the media industry, news organizations, academia, and related fields, both nationally and internationally.
