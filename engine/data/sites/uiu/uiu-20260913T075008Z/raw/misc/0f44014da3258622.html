About IAR

Fostering a culture of inquiry and innovation at UIU.

The Institute for Advanced Research (IAR) at United International University was established in 2019 with a vision to create a sustainable research ecosystem. We believe that the ranking of a university is primarily judged by its research activities and their outcomes.

IAR substantially finances multidisciplinary research projects and has spent more than 3.5 Crore BDT since its inception on direct grants alone. From 2020 onwards, UIU has collaborated with other National and International organizations to expedite jointly funded grants.

Our Mission

  • To provide statuary framework and incentives for R&D.
  • To facilitate collaboration between Academia, Industry, and Government.
  • To support faculty in publishing high-impact research in Scopus-indexed journals.
  • To solve national problems through applied research.
