Why UIU EEE?

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) at United International University is committed to providing world-class education in electrical engineering. With state-of-the-art laboratories, experienced faculty members, and strong industry connections, we prepare our students for successful careers in the ever-evolving field of electrical and electronic engineering.

Learn More About Us

Prospective Career Tracks

The EEE department offers specialized career paths aligned with industry demands, preparing students for success in cutting-edge technological fields.

Embedded Systems

Design and develop embedded systems for IoT, robotics, and automation applications with hands-on experience in microcontroller programming.

VLSI Design

Master chip design and semiconductor technology for next-generation electronic devices using industry-standard tools like Cadence.

Power Systems

Specialize in renewable energy, smart grids, and sustainable power generation for the future of energy infrastructure.

Training Centers

Our specialized training academies provide industry-ready skills and certifications.

VTA VLSI Training Academy

The VLSI Training Academy (VTA) at UIU EEE provides comprehensive training in Very Large Scale Integration design. Students gain hands-on experience with industry-standard tools like Cadence, preparing them for careers in semiconductor design and chip manufacturing.

Learn More About VTA

PETA Power & Energy Training Academy

The Power & Energy Training Academy (PETA) focuses on renewable energy systems, power electronics, and smart grid technologies. Students learn practical skills in solar PV systems, battery management, and sustainable energy solutions.

Learn More About PETA
