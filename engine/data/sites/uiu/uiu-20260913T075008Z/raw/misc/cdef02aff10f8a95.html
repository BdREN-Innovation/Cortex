WHY Data Science AT UIU

Our mission is to deliver high-quality education and hands-on practical training to prepare graduates in the field of Data Science for the technological challenges of the 21st century. We are committed to fostering students' development in learning ethics, promoting professional conduct, cultivating critical thinking skills, and honing collaborative abilities within a team setting.

Undergraduate Admission Requirements

Applicants will be required to sit for an admission test to judge their abilities and aptitude for the program. The test will be held as arranged by the university. The admission test will cover the following areas:

  • 1. Language and Communication

  • 2. Mathematics

Laboratories and Computing

There are 21 fully functional computer laboratories, each equipped with up to 35-36 Dell PCs with Intel Core-i7 processors, 16GB RAM, and 256GB-512GB SSD disk storage.

In addition to these, there are 5 (five) fully equipped hardware-based IoT and Microprocessor labs with state-of-the-art equipment readily available for teaching-learning and experiments.

Why Data Science at UIU?

The curriculum has been developed with input from local and international academic and industry experts to meet international and national benchmark requirements. The faculty pool consists of 16 full-time members with PhDs from North America, Europe, Japan, Korea, and Australia, experts in the key areas of data science and the subjects relevant to Industry 4.0.
