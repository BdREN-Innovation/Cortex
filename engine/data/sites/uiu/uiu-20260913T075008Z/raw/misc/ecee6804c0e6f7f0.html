United International University Welcome to Quest for Excellence

Welcome to United International University (UIU)

Welcome to Center for International Affairs and Cooperation (CIAC), United International University (UIU). Center for International Affairs and Cooperation (CIAC) has been established at UIU in June 2019 to address the need of internationalization that should happen and be experienced at UIU. CIAC’s aim is to provide the students, faculty members and staff the international exposures where cross-cultural diversities and practices are nurtured, and learning happens in the global scale.

Undergraduate Program

We offer several programs under School of Business and Economics(BBA, BBA in AIS), School of Science & Engineering(BSCSE, BSEEE, BSDS, BS in Civil Engineering) and School of Humanities and Social Sciences(BSEDS, BSMSJ, BSECO).
