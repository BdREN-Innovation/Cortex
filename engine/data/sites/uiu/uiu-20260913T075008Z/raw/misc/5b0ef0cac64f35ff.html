About CENTeR

Center for Emerging Networks and Technologies Research (CENTeR)

CENTeR aimed to foster research and development in the area of emerging network technologies in Bangladesh as well as to utilize the benefits of information technology for the society. The center will focus on theoretical aspects and applications of the areas of state-of-the-art networking and communication technologies to solve real-life problems towards smart living. One of the objectives of the center is to establish collaborations between academia and industry as well as provide training to make skilled professionals capable of utilizing the emerging network technologies by offering professional courses from organizations like Cisco Networking Academy, Red Hat Academy, Python Institute etc.
