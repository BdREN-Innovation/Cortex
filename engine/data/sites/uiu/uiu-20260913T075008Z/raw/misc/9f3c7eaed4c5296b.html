Our
Objectives

OBJECTIVES
  • To study political, social, economic, humanitarian, environmental, and other challenges of Sustainable Development in Bangladesh and beyond.
  • To undertake and promote research on development issues, ethnic diversities, gender, migration, locally led adaptation, water governance, food security, climate change adaptation and mitigation, locally led initiatives, and cultural studies.
  • Undertake empirical research to bridge the gap between science-policy- practice.
  • Extend international academic exchange, research coordination and collaboration to foster global network of scholars.
  • Organize national and international seminars, workshops, conferences and disseminate research outputs.
