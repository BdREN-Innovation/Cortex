Vice Chancellor’s Speech:

Welcome to United International University.

UIU is a renowned university with the mark of its magnificent progress. Various kinds of activities, academic and extra-curriculum, have brought the university to both national and international focus. The university embodies the message in its tag line – Quest for Excellence!  Our university has participation of faculty members and students in different programs to make the university a center for excellence.

The students of today are the Architects of tomorrow. To meet the challenges of the present in an ever-changing globe, teeming with bleeding-edge technology and relentless competition, the graduates of today must also be determined but flexible, dexterous but resolute. Bangladesh needs to produce graduates that will drive us to an industrial and technological renaissance and deliver us to the Promised Land. With these stated missions and resolute goals, UIU has risen to the challenge to become a significant player in the transmission and dissemination of knowledge, research, and technology.

We are currently one of the top universities in Asia according to the QS ranking. We are committed to taking the university forward and adhere to all progressive ideas as we bear the spirit of freedom, conscience, and liberal thought.

It is only a matter of time before our university finds itself in the hearts and minds of prospective students and parents alike of the near future. So, join us to help make United International University a conduit for our vision – share the united experience.

Director’s Speech

Welcome to the Institute of Business and Economics Research-IBER at United International University. The Institute offers Post Graduate programs geared towards improving capacity in public & private sectors. Our dedicated faculty members & staffs are passionate about education and research that have an impact locally and internationally. We are privileged to understand organizations, NGOs & private institutions in Bangladesh. Hence have a unique opportunity to respond to their necessities. It is why developing competent Professionals is at the top of our list of priorities.

Our degrees are world-class and cater to every sector of employees. Our degrees include PGD in Human Resource Management, PGD in Tax Management, PGD in Islamic Banking, PGD in Supply Chain Management, PGD in Capital Market Investment, Certification in Logistics & Transport. It is unique and assists, particularly professionals, to study and work at the same time. We also offer training on leadership and advanced training on Microsoft Excel.

We are producing a significant number of postgraduate diploma students in Human Resource Management in Bangladesh. IBER is partnering with national & international organizations to accommodate the further training needed.

IBER would like to invite you to study with us to become part of this journey. We will support you in realizing your full potential and aspirations.

Dr. Md. Qamruzzaman

Professor, School of Business and Economics

Director, Institute of Business and Economic Research-IBER
