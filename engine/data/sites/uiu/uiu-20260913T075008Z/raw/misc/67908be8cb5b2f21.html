NOTICE REGARDING LIBRARY AND STUDY ROOM

This is to inform all UIU students that the Shaheed Irfan Library and the study rooms on the 2nd Floor (Room 232) and Ground Floor (Room G001) will remain open on August 20, 2026 (Thursday), August 21, 2026 (Friday), August 27, 2026 (Thursday), and August 28, 2026 (Friday) from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM to facilitate preparatory studies for the Mid-term Examinations. This is to inform all UIU students that the Shaheed Irfan Library and the study room on the 2nd floor (Room 232) will remain open on June 18, 2026 (Thursday) and June 19, 2026 (Friday) as well as on June 25, 2026 (Thursday) from 8:30 AM to 9:00 PM to facilitate preparatory studies for the Final Examinations.
