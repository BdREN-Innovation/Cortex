Shadhinota Corner

Welcome to the Shadhinota Corner

The Swadhinata Corner at Shaheed Irfan Library stands as a tribute to the courage and sacrifice of Shaheed Irfan Bhuiyan during the July Revolution 2024. This dedicated space preserves historical books, records, and resources that highlight Bangladesh’s independence movement and rich history.
