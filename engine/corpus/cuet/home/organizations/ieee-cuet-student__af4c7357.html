IEEE CUET Student Branch is one of the leading student organizations at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), operating under the banner of the Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE), the world’s largest technical and professional organization dedicated to advancing technology for the benefit of humanity.

Established in 2013, IEEE CUET Student Branch has consistently worked to foster a culture of technical excellence, innovation, leadership, and professional development among CUET students. Over the years, IEEE CUET Student Branch has actively organized and contributed to a wide range of technical, professional, and innovation-driven events that reflect its commitment to excellence and student development. The branch has hosted national-level initiatives, promoting research communication and critical thinking among students. These programs have brought together students from across the country to address real-world STEM challenges through creativity and policy-driven solutions.

About IEEE

The Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) is a globally recognized professional association with more than 500,000 members across over 190 countries. 

IEEE provides a collaborative platform where students, researchers, and industry professionals work together to develop innovative solutions in fields such as electrical engineering, electronics, computer science, telecommunications, robotics, artificial intelligence, and emerging technologies. As part of this global network, IEEE CUET Student Branch enables CUET students to connect with the international engineering community and access world-class technical resources.

Who We Are

IEEE CUET Student Branch is an inclusive community of CUET undergraduate engineering students, guided by dedicated faculty mentors and led by an elected Executive Committee. Members actively organize events, lead projects, and represent CUET at national and international IEEE platforms while developing leadership, professionalism, and ethical values. The branch was honored as the Outstanding Student Branch Award 2025 by the IEEE Bangladesh Section. Beside IEEE CUET Student Branch, one Affinity Group and six Technical Societies also operate under it: IEEE WIE CUET Student Branch Affinity Group, IEEE Computer Society, IEEE Antennas and Propagation Society, IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, IEEE Power and Energy Society, IEEE Photonics Society, IEEE Signal Processing Society, and IEEE Engineering in Medicine and Biology Society. The Branch Counselor, Affinity Group Advisor and Society Advisors provide continuous guidance that strengthens academic growth and strategic direction.

Our Vision and Goals

The vision of IEEE CUET Student Branch is to create a dynamic and inclusive technical community that empowers students to innovate, lead, and make a positive impact through technology. Our goals include promoting technical excellence, encouraging research and innovation, developing ethical and effective leaders, and contributing to societal development through technology-driven solutions. We strive to inspire the next generation of engineers to think critically, act responsibly, and collaborate globally to address real-world challenges.
