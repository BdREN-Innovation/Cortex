Joyoddhoney is the only cultural organization of CUET. It was established on 16th February, 2000 by some innovative students and teachers. It staged its first program on 21st February, 2000 observing the 'Shaheed Dibash'. On 19th September it staged its first drama " Akattarer Khudiram". It is trying to keep the campus lively by arranging different cultural programs regularly.
