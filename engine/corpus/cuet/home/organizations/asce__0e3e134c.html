ASCE CUET Student Chapter, proudly affiliated with the 𝐴𝑚𝑒𝑟𝑖𝑐𝑎𝑛 𝑆𝑜𝑐𝑖𝑒𝑡𝑦 𝑜𝑓 𝐶𝑖𝑣𝑖𝑙 𝐸𝑛𝑔𝑖𝑛𝑒𝑒𝑟𝑠 (𝐴𝑆𝐶𝐸), is a vibrant student organization within Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. Our vibrant community, comprising approximately 200+ members annually from the departments of Civil Engineering, Water Resources Engineering, Urban and Regional Planning (URP), and Architecture, unites students passionate about civil engineering, sustainable infrastructure, and innovative design. This diverse membership fosters a collaborative environment for technical excellence, leadership, and professional growth.

 

As a cornerstone of CUET’s technical community, the ASCE Student Chapter CUET organizes a wide range of engaging activities, including 𝐭𝐞𝐜𝐡𝐧𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐥𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐬𝐡𝐨𝐩𝐬, 𝐞𝐱𝐩𝐞𝐫𝐭-𝐥𝐞𝐝𝐬𝐞𝐦𝐢𝐧𝐚𝐫𝐬, 𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐬𝐢𝐭𝐞𝐯𝐢𝐬𝐢𝐭𝐬to prominent infrastructure projects. Our chapter hosts impactful competitions such as the 𝐓𝐫𝐮𝐬𝐬𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧, 𝐒𝐮𝐬𝐭𝐚𝐢𝐧𝐚𝐛𝐥𝐞𝐒𝐨𝐥𝐮𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬, 𝐃𝐚𝐧𝐢𝐞𝐥𝐖. 𝐌𝐞𝐚𝐝𝐏𝐚𝐩𝐞𝐫𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧, 𝐂𝐚𝐬𝐞𝐂𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧, 𝐏𝐨𝐬𝐭𝐞𝐫𝐏𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐞𝐧𝐭𝐚𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧, and so on, enabling students to showcase their creativity, problem-solving skills, and interdisciplinary approaches. A landmark achievement for our chapter was hosting 𝑻𝒉𝒆 𝑶𝒅𝒚𝒔𝒔𝒆𝒚 2025, our inaugural national-level competition, which brought together participants from across Bangladesh to address real-world engineering challenges. These initiatives, developed in partnership with industry professionals and academic mentors, bridge theoretical knowledge with practical applications, preparing students for impactful careers.

 

Membership in the ASCE Student Chapter CUET provides access to exclusive opportunities through ASCE headquarters, including 𝐬𝐜𝐡𝐨𝐥𝐚𝐫𝐬𝐡𝐢𝐩𝐬, 𝐫𝐞𝐬𝐞𝐚𝐫𝐜𝐡𝐚𝐧𝐝𝐭𝐞𝐜𝐡𝐧𝐢𝐜𝐚𝐥𝐠𝐫𝐚𝐧𝐭𝐬, and participation in globally recognized student 𝐝𝐞𝐬𝐢𝐠𝐧𝐜𝐨𝐦𝐩𝐞𝐭𝐢𝐭𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐬. These platforms allow members to connect with 𝐚 𝐠𝐥𝐨𝐛𝐚𝐥𝐧𝐞𝐭𝐰𝐨𝐫𝐤𝐨𝐟𝐜𝐢𝐯𝐢𝐥𝐞𝐧𝐠𝐢𝐧𝐞𝐞𝐫𝐢𝐧𝐠𝐩𝐫𝐨𝐟𝐞𝐬𝐬𝐢𝐨𝐧𝐚𝐥𝐬, stay abreast of cutting-edge industry trends, and gain international exposure. Our chapter also promotes research and interdisciplinary projects, encouraging students from diverse fields to tackle pressing challenges in sustainable urban development, environmental conservation, and resilient infrastructure design.

 

With a steadfast commitment to advancing civil engineering education and sustainable practices in Bangladesh, the ASCE Student Chapter CUET empowers its members to become visionary leaders. We strive to equip the next generation of engineers, planners, and architects with the skills, knowledge, and passion to create innovative, eco-friendly solutions that shape a sustainable future for local and global communities.
