The ASHRAE CUET Student Branch is a dynamic student organization of the American Society of Heating, Refrigerating and Air-Conditioning Engineers (ASHRAE), operating under the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. It is a vibrant and forward-thinking organization that brings together students passionate about HVAC&R (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration), sustainable energy, and building systems.

As one of the most active technical student organizations in CUET, the ASHRAE CUET Student Branch regularly hosts seminars, workshops, technical talks, industrial visits, and student competitions in collaboration with industry experts and academic professionals. These events aim to promote practical knowledge, technological innovation, and professional development among CUET’s engineering students.
