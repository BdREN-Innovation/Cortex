CUET Journalist Association (shortly known as CUETJA) is the organization of journalists studying in CUET. The members of this organization work in the several national newspapers of Bangladesh. It was established on 16th December, 2006. From the beginning it plays its robust rule with unprecedented reputation in different sectors. It has launched a campus-based 24 hours online newspaper called cuetnews24.com. It also organizes CUETJA AWAKENING FESTIVAL to make a platform of the intellectual and mass people to unbar their intellect thoughts.
