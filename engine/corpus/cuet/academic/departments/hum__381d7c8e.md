## About

The department of Humanities of CUET is a non degree awarding department. But it plays a vital role in teaching the engineering students the essential Accounting, Management, Literature, Economics, Sociology, Psychology etc.

## Vision

The department of Humanities has set its goal to impart interdisciplinary knowledge, skills, and values to students in order to contribute to their academic and professional development. The vision of The Department of Humanities is to deliver the learning outcomes specified by the study program and achieve excellence in the fields of higher education by virtue of extensive knowledge on associated subject matter, comprehensive curriculum and consistent enhancement of teaching.

## Mission

The mission of the department of Humanities (HUM) is to provide opportunities for the undergraduate engineering students to develop their linguistic competence, accountability, economic excellence, and human morality, social and cultural values in order to contribute to the Sustainable Development of Bangladesh as well as rest of the world.

## Message from the Head

The Department of Humanities contributes significantly to the university’s mission of academic excellence by providing students with strong intellectual, ethical, and communicative foundations. Since CUET’s evolution from Engineering College, Chittagong in 1968 to a full-fledged university in 2003, the Department of Humanities has remained integral to fostering holistic and well-rounded academic and professional development. The department offers a range of multidisciplinary courses drawn from the streams of arts, humanities, business and social sciences, including English, Economics, Sociology and Accounting. These courses are designed for undergraduate students of engineering, architecture and planning departments to enhance their proficiency in communication, critical thinking, ethical reasoning, leadership, and socio-cultural awareness. By integrating humanistic perspectives with technical education, the Department of Humanities aims to equip CUET students with the intellectual depth, moral values, and global outlook necessary to apply their engineering knowledge effectively in addressing real-world challenges. In alignment with CUET’s commitment to innovation, research, and national development, the department contributes to shaping socially responsible, ethically grounded, and globally competent engineering professionals.
