About

Welcome to the Department of Nuclear Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), a pioneering institution dedicated to advancing knowledge and expertise in nuclear engineering and technology within the context of Bangladesh. Our department assumes a pivotal role in shaping future leaders in the field, offering comprehensive academic programs spanning from undergraduate to doctoral levels. With an unwavering commitment to academic excellence, research, and societal service, we aspire to make significant contributions to the development of Bangladesh's nuclear infrastructure. Explore our website to gain insights into our educational offerings, research endeavors, and the accomplished faculty and students who constitute our vibrant academic community. Join us in the collective pursuit of excellence in nuclear engineering at CUET. In addressing the challenges of the 21st century and aligning with the government's vision to advance Bangladesh, Nuclear Engineering assumes a pivotal role. Professionals in this domain focus on harnessing energy from nuclear reactions. Bangladesh is progressively incorporating nuclear engineering for diverse applications, spanning power generation, medicine, and industrial and agricultural processes. The effective management of nuclear infrastructure and materials necessitates the presence of skilled professionals. The initiation of nuclear engineering and technology education at CUET fulfils this imperative, aligning with CUET's distinguished role in higher education, research, and development in engineering and applied sciences in Bangladesh. The Department of Nuclear Engineering (NE) at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) was established in February 2019 under the esteemed faculty of Engineering and Technology.

Vision

The Nuclear Engineering Department at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology aims to excel globally in higher education for the peaceful applications of nuclear energy while serving as a centre of excellence in education, research, and technical support and creating man power for the country's nuclear power program.

Mission

  • Academic Excellence: Cultivate highly proficient nuclear engineers across educational levels, from undergraduate to postgraduate, empowering them to contribute substantive engineering acumen to the design, construction, and operation of Bangladesh's nuclear power plants. Fostering a diverse and inclusive community of scholars, professionals and students dedicated to advancing the peaceful and beneficial uses of nuclear technology for the betterment of society.
  • Centre of Academic Distinction: Strive to achieve recognition as a distinguished academic centre for nuclear engineering and technology, encompassing nuclear power, nuclear medicine, and nuclear applications in agriculture. Dedicate efforts to provide exceptional education, conduct impactful research, and extend meaningful outreach initiatives. The centre also offers technical expertise and professional support to advance Bangladesh’s nuclear infrastructure, promote the safe and effective use of nuclear power in the national energy landscape, enhance healthcare through nuclear medicine, and support sustainable agricultural development through peaceful nuclear technologies.
  • Nuclear Research Facility Development: Committed to the development of a modern Nuclear Research Facility to support advanced education, cutting-edge research, and innovation in nuclear science and technology. The facility will enhance capabilities in nuclear power, radiation applications, nuclear medicine, and agricultural technologies while ensuring the highest standards of safety and regulatory compliance.
  • Public and Professional Engagement: Undertake service initiatives for industry, government, professional bodies, and the wider public, focusing on areas pertinent to nuclear and radiological engineering. Promoting nuclear safety, security, and non-proliferation through rigorous research, education, and outreach efforts.

Message from the Head

As the Head of the Department of Nuclear Engineering at the Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), I have the honour of overseeing a distinguished assembly of educators and scholars. The establishment of the Department of Nuclear Engineering signifies a deliberate response to the escalating demand for proficient engineers and researchers, particularly within the increasing field of nuclear-related activities, specifically in power generation. Our department is dedicated in its commitment to shaping aspiring individuals into expert technical leaders, thereby contributing to the cultivation of skilled professionals in the domain of nuclear engineering. Dedicated to delivering a comprehensive education in Nuclear Engineering, our curriculum ensures a robust foundation and offers specialized studies in Nuclear and Reactor Physics, Nuclear Reactor and System Design, Nuclear Power Plant Engineering, and Nuclear Fuel Cycle. We emphasize the importance of a well-rounded education for success in the field, preparing students for national and international excellence. A key objective of our department is to foster advanced studies and research in nuclear engineering for postgraduate (Diploma), M.S., M.Sc., and Ph.D. degrees, complemented by structured training and post-doctoral research. Our programs equip individuals with skills and knowledge relevant to nuclear power generation, nuclear medicine, nuclear food preservation, and applications in construction and workforce industries. In addition to our educational endeavours, we are dedicated to advancing Nuclear Engineering knowledge through ongoing research projects. Actively seeking collaborations with industrial partners, government agencies, and academic institutions, we aim to be a global centre of excellence in nuclear engineering, contributing to effective and peaceful applications in power generation, nuclear medicine, agriculture, and industry. As the Head of the Department of Nuclear Engineering, I eagerly anticipate collaborating with our faculty, staff, students, and industrial partners to further our vision of excellence.
