About

The Department of Chemistry at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) is a vibrant academic and research community dedicated to excellence in chemistry education, fundamental and applied research, innovation, and technological development. We strive to create an inspiring and inclusive environment where students, faculty, researchers, and staff can explore new ideas, develop their potential, and contribute meaningfully to academia, industry, and society.

With a history spanning more than five decades, the Department has played an important role in providing students from various engineering disciplines with a strong foundation in chemistry—the central science that connects engineering, technology, medicine, agriculture, environmental science, materials science, and many other fields. Our undergraduate chemistry education emphasizes both fundamental concepts and practical laboratory skills, enabling students to understand the principles of chemistry and apply them to real-world technological and engineering challenges.

The Department is committed not only to teaching but also to advancing knowledge through high-quality research and innovation. Our research activities cover a broad spectrum of contemporary areas, including environmental chemistry, natural product chemistry, nanochemistry, materials science, computational chemistry, biological chemistry, surface science, polymer science, electrochemistry, theoretical chemistry, and other emerging interdisciplinary fields. Through these activities, we seek to address important scientific, environmental, technological, and societal challenges at both national and global levels.

Graduate and Postgraduate Education

The Department expanded its academic portfolio by introducing M.Phil. and Ph.D. programs in Chemistry in 2009, providing opportunities for advanced research and the development of future leaders in chemical sciences. Subsequently, the M.Sc. program was introduced in 2017, further strengthening the Department's graduate education and research capacity.

Our graduate and postgraduate programs are designed for students who are passionate about chemistry and motivated to pursue advanced scientific research. Students have opportunities to work closely with experienced faculty members in interdisciplinary research environments, develop advanced experimental and analytical skills, publish scientific research, and contribute to the development of innovative solutions to contemporary challenges.

Research, Innovation, and Collaboration

We believe that the future of chemistry lies at the intersection of fundamental science, engineering, technology, and innovation. Therefore, the Department encourages interdisciplinary research and collaboration with academic institutions, industries, government organizations, and national and international research communities.

Our goal is to transform scientific knowledge into practical solutions in areas such as sustainable materials, environmental protection, clean water, emerging contaminants, advanced oxidation processes, energy, nanotechnology, and other areas of strategic importance.

Commitment to Industry and Society

In addition to our academic and research activities, the Department contributes to industry and society through professional testing, analytical, and consultancy services. These activities provide technical support to industries, organizations, researchers, and other stakeholders while creating opportunities for our faculty and researchers to engage with real-world scientific and technological challenges.

We consider our engagement with industry and society an integral part of our mission. It enables us to translate academic expertise into practical solutions while continuously strengthening our capacity to address the evolving technological challenges of the twenty-first century.

Looking Ahead

As we move forward, the Department of Chemistry remains committed to building a center of excellence in chemistry education, research, and innovation. We aim to nurture scientifically competent, creative, ethical, and globally competitive graduates and researchers who can contribute to sustainable technological development and address the challenges facing Bangladesh and the world.

We warmly welcome students, researchers, academics, industry partners, and collaborators to join our journey of learning, discovery, innovation, and service.

Vision

To be a nationally and internationally recognized center of excellence in chemistry education, research, and innovation, producing highly skilled, ethical, and creative graduates and researchers who contribute to scientific advancement, technological innovation, environmental sustainability, and the socio-economic development of Bangladesh and the global community.

Mission

The Department of Chemistry, CUET is committed to:

Providing high-quality, innovative, and student-centered education in chemistry that develops strong theoretical knowledge, practical skills, critical thinking, and problem-solving abilities.

Promoting cutting-edge research and innovation in fundamental and applied chemistry to address emerging scientific, technological, environmental, and societal challenges.

Developing competent and ethical professionals, researchers, and future leaders equipped with the knowledge, skills, creativity, and integrity required to contribute effectively to academia, industry, research organizations, and society.

Advancing interdisciplinary and collaborative research by fostering national and international partnerships with academic institutions, industries, government organizations, and research communities.

Promoting sustainable chemistry and environmentally responsible technologies to address challenges related to clean water, environmental pollution, energy, climate change, health, and sustainable development.

Encouraging innovation, entrepreneurship, and technology transfer to transform scientific knowledge and research outcomes into practical solutions for national and global needs.

Creating an inclusive, safe, and intellectually stimulating academic environment that encourages lifelong learning, scientific curiosity, creativity, collaboration, and professional development.

Contributing to national development and the advancement of society through excellence in education, research, innovation, knowledge dissemination, and community engagement.

Message from the Head

Welcome to the Department of Chemistry at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET).

Chemistry is often described as the central science because it connects and contributes to almost every aspect of our lives. From the materials we use and the medicines we depend on to clean water, energy, agriculture, environmental protection, and advanced technologies, chemistry is fundamental to understanding and shaping the world around us. In an era of rapid scientific and technological transformation, chemistry continues to serve as a powerful driving force behind innovations in engineering, medicine, agriculture, environmental science, energy, and materials technology.

The Department of Chemistry at CUET is committed to excellence in education, research, innovation, and the development of skilled human resources. The Department provides a stimulating academic and research environment for undergraduate students from different engineering disciplines, as well as graduate and postgraduate students. We strive to develop a strong foundation in chemical sciences while encouraging students to think critically, explore creatively, and apply their knowledge to real-world challenges.

The Department offers M.Sc., M.Phil., and Ph.D. programs in Chemistry, providing opportunities for advanced study and research across a broad range of contemporary and interdisciplinary areas. Our research encompasses environmental chemistry, natural product chemistry, nanochemistry, materials science, computational chemistry, biological chemistry, surface science, polymer science, electrochemistry, theoretical chemistry, and other emerging fields. Through these research activities, we aim to generate new knowledge and develop innovative solutions to pressing national and global challenges.

Our greatest strength is our dedicated faculty members, researchers, and staff, who are highly qualified, enthusiastic, cooperative, and committed to academic and research excellence. We believe that a supportive and inclusive academic environment is essential for nurturing curiosity, creativity, integrity, and scientific excellence. Accordingly, we encourage close interaction among students, faculty, and researchers and promote interdisciplinary collaboration both within CUET and with national and international institutions.

As a department within an engineering-focused university, we particularly value the integration of chemistry with engineering and technology. Our goal is not only to teach chemistry but also to empower our students and researchers to translate chemical knowledge into practical technologies and sustainable solutions. We are committed to addressing contemporary challenges such as environmental pollution, emerging contaminants, clean water, sustainable materials, energy, public health, and climate change through innovative research and collaboration.

We warmly welcome students, researchers, academics, industries, and collaborators to engage with our Department. Whether you are seeking a strong academic foundation, an opportunity for advanced research, interdisciplinary collaboration, or a platform for innovation, we hope you will find the Department of Chemistry at CUET an inspiring place to learn, discover, and contribute.

Together, we aspire to nurture a generation of scientifically skilled engineers, chemists, researchers, and innovators who will create meaningful discoveries, develop sustainable technologies, and contribute to a better future for Bangladesh and the world.

Once again, welcome to the Department of Chemistry at CUET. We look forward to learning, discovering, and innovating together.
