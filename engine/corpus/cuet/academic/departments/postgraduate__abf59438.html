Postgraduate Studies

 

Degree Offered: M.Sc. Engg (NE) / M. Engg (NE)

Total Credit: 36 Credit hours

Theory Course

  • 18 Credit hours [M. Sc. Engg, (NE)]
  • 30 Credit hours [M. Engg, (NE)]

Thesis

  • 18 Credit hours [M. Sc. Engg, (NE)]
  • 6 Credit hours [M. Engg, (NE)]

Duration

  • 3-Semesters for full-time students [2 (Two) semesters in each academic year]
  • 4-Semesters for part-time students [2 (Two) semesters in each academic year]

Each Semester

  • Theory Courses + Thesis = Maximum 12 credits (full-time students)
  • Theory Courses + Thesis = Maximum 9 credits (part-time students)

Total Number of Theory Courses

  • 6 courses (Each theory course: 3 credits) [M. Sc. Engg, (NE)]
  • 10 courses (Each theory course: 3 credits) [M. Engg, (NE)]
