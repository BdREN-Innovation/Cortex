##  Postgraduate Study Programs

The postgraduate curriculum for the Department of Mathematics at CUET is designed to provide students with advanced theoretical knowledge, computational expertise, and research-oriented skills to solve complex mathematical problems. This program emphasizes a strong foundation in core areas such as real and complex analysis, algebra, and differential equations, while offering specialized electives in emerging fields like data science, optimization, and cryptography. With a focus on interdisciplinary applications, students engage in hands-on training, industry collaborations, and rigorous research, preparing them for careers in academia, industry, or further research pursuits. The curriculum fosters critical thinking, innovation, and a passion for lifelong learning in mathematical sciences.
