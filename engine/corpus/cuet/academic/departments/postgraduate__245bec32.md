##  Postgraduate Study Programs

The postgraduate program has a vigorous, rapidly expanding program of advanced study and research in the areas of Power system planning, Energy conversion, Optical fiber communication, Digital signal processing, Power semiconductor circuits, Industrial drives, MOS devices, Microwave theory and techniques, Laser theory, Artificial intelligence, and neural networks, etc. These postgraduate programs lead to the degree of Master of Science in Electrical and Electronic Engineering and Doctor of Philosophy in Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

To be awarded an M. Sc. degree, a student must complete 36 credit hours, of which a maximum of 09 credit hours are for project work or 18 credit hours are for thesis work. The degree requirement for a Ph.D. is 60 credit hours, of which a maximum of 45 credit hours are for the thesis.

### Research Facilities

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering has many advanced instruments and equipment with laboratories for studying various Electrical and Electronic Engineering problems.

#### Research Laboratory: Advanced Power System Laboratory

Objectives:

1. How to design and perform highly detailed simulation of power systems, and emulate the behavior of power system devices in different time scales.
1. How to conduct research and educational activities in the areas of Smart Grids with the changing environment resulting from the dramatic increase in deployments of renewable and distributed energy.
1. How to carry on distributed control and information exchange in the power system elements using compatible communication protocols (e.g., IEC61850).

#### Renewable Energy Lab: Solar Energy - Thin Film Solar Cells

Solar cells are a renewable way of producing electricity from sunlight without wearing out or consuming anything. Thin-film solar cells are the new generation of solar cells containing multiple thin-film layers of photovoltaic materials. The thin-film layers are deposited on a glass or plastic substrate, offering promising options for reducing photovoltaic system costs and increasing solar electricity market penetration.
