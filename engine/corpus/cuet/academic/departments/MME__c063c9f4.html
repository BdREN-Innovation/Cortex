About

The Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering (MME) at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) was established in the 2019-2020 academic session with the goal of fostering excellence in the fields of materials science and metallurgy. Beginning its journey with just 30 undergraduate students, the department has continued to evolve and expand, maintaining a strong commitment to producing skilled engineers who are equipped with both theoretical knowledge and practical expertise. The purpose of the MME Department is to prepare students for the challenges of modern industry by providing a comprehensive understanding of materials—from their fundamental properties to their applications in real-world engineering problems. The department strives to bridge the gap between material science and engineering applications, ensuring that students develop the necessary skills to innovate, design, and optimize materials for a variety of industrial sectors.

Vision

The vision of the MME Department is to become a leading hub for education and research in materials and metallurgical engineering, producing graduates who are capable of making significant contributions to technological advancements and innovation in the field. The department envisions empowering students to drive the future of industries by mastering the complexities of materials science and metallurgy while adhering to global standards and sustainable practices.
Each year, the department admits 30 students per batch for the B.Sc. in Materials and Metallurgical Engineering (MME), ensuring a focused and hands-on learning environment where students receive personalized attention and are well-prepared for their careers. Through a rigorous curriculum, cutting-edge research, and a focus on practical skills, the MME Department at CUET is shaping the next generation of materials engineers.

Mission

The mission of the MME Department at CUET is to:

Provide high-quality education in materials and metallurgical engineering, focusing on both the theoretical foundations and practical applications.
Foster research and innovation in the areas of materials processing, characterization, and advanced manufacturing.
Equip students with the necessary tools to contribute to sustainable development through the responsible use of materials in industries like aerospace, automotive, energy, and electronics.
Encourage interdisciplinary collaboration and teamwork among students to solve complex engineering problems.

Laboratories

The Department of Materials and Metallurgical Engineering (MME) is equipped with a comprehensive suite of modern laboratories that support both teaching and research across a wide spectrum of materials science and engineering. These laboratories—including the Nano-fabrication Lab, Surface Engineering Lab, Materials Characterization Lab, Glass and Ceramics Lab, Wet Synthesis Lab, Polymer and Composite Lab, Metallography Lab, High Temperature Processing Lab, Materials Testing Lab, and HPC & Simulation Lab—provide an integrated platform for understanding materials from fundamental principles to advanced applications.

Through a combination of experimental facilities and computational tools, students and researchers gain hands-on experience in materials synthesis, processing, characterization, testing, and simulation. The laboratories are designed to bridge theory and practice, enabling the exploration of structure–property–performance relationships across multiple length scales.
