## About

Today, we are at the peak of the information technology age, and communication engineering plays a vital role in today's rapidly changing world. In this prospect, CUET has launched Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) in the year 2012. The vision of this department is to make great contribution by producing efficient and resourceful engineers with research and development capabilities.

The ETE Department has well qualified and experienced faculty members with areas of specialization that include Embedded Systems, Applied Electronics and VLSI design. The department has taken step to collect technologically advanced equipment and to build enriched laboratories. Students will be given vast amount of practical exposure through the use of innovative methodologies in the design and development of electronics, which is the backbone for all engineering disciplines.

The Department of ETE offers a comprehensive range of rigorous, innovative programs .The undergraduate curriculum of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering: is designed to give students a sound knowledge of Engineering fundamentals, strong physical sciences background and adequate practical training so that they will be ready to quickly achieve competence in treating current technical problems as well as those that will come with the rapidly changing technologies of the year-to-come. The Electronic and Telecommunication Engineering program prepares students for careers in such areas of electronics, computer, communication etc.

The aim of the undergraduate program of the Department of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE) is to provide the students with a technical and engineering background and scientific research capabilities in the design and development and production of electronic devices, circuits and systems used in a wide spectrum of applications ranging from home appliances to the most sophisticated satellite communications.

## Laboratories

To develop practical skills necessary for study and research, the ETE department has well-equipped and modern laboratories enriched with advanced training modules and equipment. Through experimentation, research, and exploration in the following laboratories of the ETE department, students can enrich their knowledge.

### Electronic Device & Circuit Laboratory

In order to gain fundamental and practical knowledge of electronics engineering, this well-equipped laboratory is used, which is enriched with modern electronic instruments and devices. This laboratory also serves students for implementing their projects and thesis in different terms.

The laboratory is well-equipped with:

- Ammeter
- Voltmeter
- Multimeter
- Oscilloscope (Analog and Digital)
- AC & DC Power Supply Unit and Variable AC Source
- Function Generator
- LCR Meter
- Other relevant components and semiconductor devices extensively used in electronics

### Antenna & Microwave Engineering Laboratory

One of the major sectors of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering is Antenna & Microwave Engineering. This laboratory provides students with all modern, necessary training kits and software for conducting their practical courses and advanced research in this field. This lab facilitates students with the following:

- Wave and Antenna Training System (WATS-2002)
- Microwave Communication Trainer (MW-2000)
- Microwave Signal Generation, Modulation, Transmission, Reception Trainer (ED-3000)
- ADS Software
- 9 GHz Vector Network Analyzer

### Simulation Laboratory

In this laboratory, students can perform computer programming and numerical analysis lab, signals and systems lab, digital signal processing lab, multimedia communication lab, neural and fuzzy systems lab in C, C++, MATLAB, and Python to learn about different types of signal generation, manipulation of audio & video signals, spectral analysis, filter response, computer programming, etc. Besides, design and simulation of different logic circuits in the field of VLSI is done in this laboratory by Cadence Virtuoso Software. In internet programming, students learn about HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP and create databases for website development.

### Communication System Laboratory

Communication, the core of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering, is one that requires hands-on experience and practical demonstrations of systems in communication. Our Communication System Laboratory enables students to conduct their analog and digital communication lab, telecommunication networks, and switching lab with all the latest communication trainers. The lab provides the following trainers:

- Mobile Telecom Trainer
- Telecommunication Switching Trainer
- EPABX Trainer
- GSM Trainer
- GPS Trainer
- Analog Communication Trainer
- Digital Communication Trainer

### Computer Communications & Network Laboratory

To visualize the networking environments of communication systems, this well-resourced laboratory is equipped with LAN trainer, different types of networking devices, Cisco Packet Tracer, and Network Analyzer software, which are used to simulate and analyze network topologies, and configure Cisco routers and switches.

### Digital & Microprocessor Laboratory

In the Digital & Microprocessor Laboratory, students conduct experiments on digital logic circuits and also learn assembly language programming, generate hex code, and input it to the 8086 microprocessor kit. Our Digital & Microprocessor Laboratory provides the following setup:

- Digital Electronics Trainer Board
- Different types of Logic ICs
- MIDAS-8086 Microprocessor
- Microcontroller
- FPGA Trainer

### Optical Fiber Communication Laboratory

Optical Fiber Communication has revolutionized the telecommunications industry with its enormous advantages. Our Optical Fiber Communication Laboratory is enriched with the latest trainers like fiber optic analog and digital communication trainers, modulation and demodulation trainers, co-axial transmission line trainers, etc. to facilitate students' practical understanding.

### Biomedical Instrumentation Laboratory

The Biomedical Instrumentation Laboratory is for conducting experiments in the field of biomedical engineering. This laboratory includes a transducer trainer to observe several biomedical sensors' performance, ECG, EEG, EMG trainers to visualize their characteristics, and a Digital Electrocardiograph module to monitor real-time ECG.

Students in the first year have to undertake laboratory/sessional classes in Physics, Chemistry, and Electrical Circuit Laboratory. Initially, students will make use of all the laboratory facilities from the Department of EEE and the Department of CSE.
