About

Department of Architecture has marked a progression in the history of Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology which has been established in 2009 under the Faculty of Architecture & Planning. The Department of Architecture has been established alongside the existing engineering education. The Department of Architecture is committed to advance architectural education through different programs and researches. The department continuously endeavors to instill in the students a firm intellectual and professional base. The course curricula are designed to bring out the best in students aspiring to enter the profession. The University Library has a section for the Department of Architecture, which is very rich in providing essential reading material for the Undergraduate course.

Vision

To be a center of excellence in architectural education, research and innovation, fostering sustainable and context-responsive design solutions that shape the built environment for a better future.

Mission

The Architecture Department of CUET is committed to providing quality education through a comprehensive and dynamic curriculum that equips students with the knowledge, creativity, and technical skills essential for architectural practice. We emphasize sustainable and contextual design, promoting environmentally responsible and socially relevant architecture that addresses both global and local challenges. Through interdisciplinary research, technological advancements, and innovation, we strive to contribute to the evolving fields of architecture and urban planning. Our mission also focuses on professional and ethical development, instilling strong values, leadership qualities, and a deep sense of responsibility towards society and the profession. Furthermore, we actively engage with industries, government, and communities to foster collaborations that contribute to the sustainable development of the built environment.

Message from the Head

The Department of Architecture is founded on strong principles of multidisciplinary collaboration and respect for experimentation, rooted in social, cultural, and environmental values. We are trying to reflect in our innovative teaching, scholarly research, critical design inquiry, and synthetic practice. These principles will remain a strong reinforcement of our school, while supporting our incessant exploration of emerging theory and knowledge in design thinking, innovative materials and technologies, digital design and fabrication, and sustainability. Our undergraduate program covers fields of design and design theory, history of architecture and urbanism, building science and sustainability, structures and construction, social and cultural processes, environmental design in developing country like ours, digital design and virtual environments, urban design, and visual studies. We are interested in the entire life cycle of the building process, from conceptualization, through design and representation, craft and construction, and the performance of both constructed and virtual environments. We teach students to imagine, question, experiment, innovate, build, and reflect. Our curriculum is designed to foster independent thinking and to give students a balance of foundational knowledge and skills, with flexibility to follow their passions. We look forward to a busy and productive year of continued excellence. The Department of Architecture is closely linked with other departments, notably engineering. All of the Department's activities are accommodated on one site, including: design studio spaces, lecture and classrooms, workshop and IT facilities, research offices, staff offices and administration. The Department adopts a range of teaching methods and covers a wide range of topics from sustainable design to cultural studies.
