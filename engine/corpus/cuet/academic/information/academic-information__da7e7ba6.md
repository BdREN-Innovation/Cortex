## Welcome to the Department of Civil Engineering, CUET

The **Department of Civil Engineering** at **Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)** offers a comprehensive undergraduate program designed to equip students with the knowledge, skills, and practical experience required to excel in the field of civil engineering.

Civil engineering is fundamental to the development and sustainability of modern infrastructure. As urbanization and industrial growth accelerate, the demand for competent civil engineers continues to rise. Our program provides students with a strong foundation in key areas, including:

- **Structural Engineering**
- **Geotechnical Engineering**
- **Environmental Engineering**
- **Transportation Engineering**
- **Water Resources Engineering**

### Curriculum and Learning Approach

The undergraduate curriculum is continuously updated to reflect evolving industry requirements, ensuring that graduates are well-prepared to address contemporary engineering challenges. In addition to technical proficiency, the program emphasizes:

- Problem-solving and analytical thinking
- Critical reasoning and decision-making
- Professional ethics and sustainable engineering practices

### Key Features of the Undergraduate Program

1. **Well-Structured Curriculum**
Core courses, specialized electives, and project-based learning provide comprehensive coverage of civil engineering disciplines.
1. **State-of-the-Art Laboratories**
Students have access to advanced facilities for structural analysis, material testing, surveying, geotechnical testing, and environmental studies.
1. **Experienced Faculty**
The department is supported by a team of highly qualified faculty members with extensive academic and industry experience.
1. **Hands-On Learning**
Fieldwork, internships, and collaborative industry projects provide students with practical, real-world experience.
1. **Research Opportunities**
Students are encouraged to participate in cutting-edge research in areas such as sustainable construction, smart infrastructure, and green technologies.

**Industry Collaboration**
Strong partnerships with leading civil engineering firms and organizations facilitate internships, professional exposure, and career development opportunities.

## Undergraduate Study Programs

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) is one of the largest and most prestigious departments at this university, with a rich history of excellence in undergraduate and postgraduate teaching and research. The department is dedicated to producing top-tier engineers, educators, and professionals for both national and international arenas. To keep pace with the evolving demands of the 21st century, the EEE department regularly upgrades its course curricula, expands laboratory facilities, and revises teaching and research materials. The undergraduate syllabus, which is available at the department office, is meticulously designed by experienced faculty members, incorporating feedback from alumni and insights from renowned international faculty members.

----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The Bachelor of Science in Electrical and Electronic Engineering curriculum provides comprehensive coverage of fundamental Electrical and Electronic Technology principles, emphasizing their application in achieving optimal design solutions for engineering challenges. In the first year, students focus on foundational subjects such as Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Economics, Basic Mechanical Engineering, and Basic Electrical Engineering. The subsequent three years delve into advanced topics, including Engineering Mathematics, Electrical Circuits, Electrical Machines and Drives, Analog and Digital Electronics, Power Electronics, Electromagnetic Fields, Programming Techniques, Power Transmission and Distribution, Electrical Measurement and Instrumentation, Control System Engineering, Communication Engineering, Switchgear and Protection, Power Station and Economy, Microwave Engineering and Antenna Design, Microprocessors and Microcomputers, VLSI, Signal Processing, and other emerging areas of Electrical and Electronic Engineering. The curriculum integrates theoretical courses, hands-on laboratory work, industrial training, and field trips to provide students with a well-rounded educational experience.

## Undergraduate Study Programs

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering offers a comprehensive streamlined curriculum at undergraduate level, which needs 160 credit hours including 4 credit hours in thesis and project work to be completed for achieving bachelor degree. The well thought and full-fledged curriculum allows students greater flexibility and provides experiential learning. This program begins with core courses and ends with specialization in a particular area through thesis and project work under a faculty supervisor. During the first two years, students take compulsory courses in the core areas of computer science and engineering as well as in mathematics and other engineering disciplines. In the third and fourth year, the students concentrate in a number of advanced courses in computer science and engineering providing deeper and applied knowledge and greater ability to take part in the development of different computer system hardware and software. The syllabus of the department updates regularly with recent development of knowledge and technology in the relevant field of study. The current syllabus has updated in 2017 and effective for the session 2017-18 and onward. The major inclusion in the updated curriculum is the introduction of various software development projects in second and third year. The minimum CGPA requirement for being awarded of Bachelor of Science degree in Computer Science and Engineering is 2.25 out of 4.00.
