About

The Center for Industrial Problems Research (CIPR) was formed as a research center at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology according to the decision granted at the 97th meeting of the Academic Council held on 06-04-2016. The institute was established first time in the country with a view to connect industries with faculty, postdocs, and graduate students who have expertise in industrial case studies. The center aims to interact between industry and academia in such a way that academics learn about interesting potential research problems and find applications for their existing tools. Industries get access to some of the most experienced researchers and problem-solvers in the country.

Message from the Head

The Center for Industrial Problems Research (CIPR) will work as a Center of Excellence committed to conduct researches in the field of Industrial Problems and Safety to the highest level. This Center will take initiatives to establish internal and external collaboration with relevant Institutes/ Centers in home and abroad.
