## About

The Center for Environmental Science & Engineering Research (CESER) is the oldest center of CUET focuses the research on all aspect of Environmental Science and Engineering topics in the context to Global Environmental Challenges and Sustainable Development. In this regard, the center is working for developing local environmental manpower and expertise in order to enhance the economic growth of the country without damaging the quality of the environment, to promote and provide facilities for offering consultation services to development agencies and partners in matters of environmental relevance. The center is committed to develop an environmental management information system (EMIS) within the center to facilitate collection and dissemination of information and training materials to institutions that train environmental professionals. The center is also working to help in formulating the codes of practices for a cleaner and greener environment.

## Vision

**Aims & Objectives of the Center **

The overall aim of the Center is to create a Center of Excellence with state of art research facilities and study in the area of Environmental Science and Engineering. In this regard the followings shall be the objectives of the Center:

a) To promote and provide facilities for
i. Post graduate diploma and certificate programs for the professionals
ii. Advanced studies and research facilities for students, teachers and professionals
iii. Developing local environmental manpower and expertise in order to enhance the economic growth of the country without damaging the quality of the environment
iv. Mainstreaming environmental dimension within the processes of planning, design, implementation, operation and maintenance of development projects
v. Developing capacity for environmental research in order to support environmentally sound development programs in the country
vi. Training and education to technologist, managers, decision makers, academician and other concerned officials in sound management approaches, parallel to technological and least cost solutions to the environmental problems
vii. Providing specific project oriented training to professionals of industries and other development agencies within the energy sector
viii. Offering consultation services to development agencies and partners in matters of environmental relevance
ix. Conducting research in areas of global and national environmental problems, and
x. Developing an environmental management information system (EMIS) within the center to facilitate collection and dissemination of information and training materials to institutions that train environmental professionals.

b) To provide necessary forum for professional engineers and policy & decision makers to exchange ideas in the field of environmental science and engineering both at home and abroad

c) To help in formulating the codes of practices for cleaner and greener environment:
i. Bangladesh Standards for environmental quality (e.g. air quality, water quality, wastewater quality, noise quality, sediment quality and etc.)
ii. Bangladesh Standard Code of Practice for plumbing system for residential commercial and industrial building
iii. Bangladesh Standard Code of Practice for water and wastewater treatment plant design, and
iv. Bangladesh Standard Code of Practice for Environmental Impact assessment

## Mission

**Mission**
The Center for Environmental Science and Engineering Research (CESER) will be established to provide world class research facilities to achieve “Center of Excellence”. It would be guided by an Advisory Committee of leading environmental practitioners and is committed to conduct research at advanced level. The Center is expected to create state-of-the-art research facilities covering all aspect of Environmental Science and Engineering topics in the context to Global Environmental Challenges.
The Center is also committed to develop curriculum, code, standard, guideline and regulation for a safer and cleaner environment. The center will also be involved in the public awareness building program in relation to Environmental issues subjected to national and international interest.

## Message from the Head

Environmental science is an interdisciplinary academic field that integrates physical, chemical, biological and information sciences to the study of the environment and the solution of environmental problems. Environmental engineering focuses on design and technology to improve environmental quality in everyday life. Now-a –days global warming is a buzz issue that is caused due to anthropogenic environmental pollution. With the rapid changes, there is a need to study each step that may alter the environment so that the natural ecosystem may still be protected or replaced by a better one. The purpose of this center is to strengthen environmental governance that will lead to the adoption of improved environmental practices, technologies, and services, particularly in addressing air pollution, water and waste management challenges.
