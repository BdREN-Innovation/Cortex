Welcome to my academic and professional hub, where science meets innovation to address some of the world’s most pressing environmental challenges. I am Dr. Yunus Ahmed, a Professor in the Department of Chemistry (Branch: Chemical Engineering) at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. With over two decades of experience in research, teaching, and leadership, my work focuses on advancing knowledge and developing practical solutions in environmental science, water treatment, antimicrobial resistance, and nanomaterial development.

This website serves as a platform to share my research contributions, academic journey, and collaborative projects with students, colleagues, and the global scientific community. Whether you’re here to explore cutting-edge research, seek mentorship, or collaborate on impactful projects, I hope this site provides valuable insights into my work and inspires meaningful engagement.
