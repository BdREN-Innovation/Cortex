Fahim Shahriar is a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). He joined the department in May 2025 and specializes in the field of Structural Engineering. He is also a member of the Bureau of Research, Testing and Consultation (BRTC), Department of Civil Engineering, CUET. Prior to joining CUET, he completed his internship at the Local Government Engineering Department (LGED), where he gained practical experience in civil infrastructure development.

During his undergraduate studies, he secured First Position with Honors in his B.Sc. in Civil Engineering and was also awarded Merit Awards in recognition of his academic excellence. He is currently pursuing his M.Sc. in Civil Engineering with a specialization in Structural Engineering. His academic and research interests include structural analysis, structural design, and innovative construction practices.
