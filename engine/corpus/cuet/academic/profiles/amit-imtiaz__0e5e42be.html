Amit Imtiaz, University of Sheffield (UK) Graduate, currently working as an Assistant Professor at CUET. Mr. Imtiaz started his career as an Assistant Architect at Volumezero Limited after his graduation. At the end of 2016, he joined as a lecturer at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology, from where he graduated as well. Mr. Imtiaz completed his MSc in Sustainable Architecture Studies in 2023 from the School of Architecture, University of Sheffield, United Kingdom. For this study Mr. Imtiaz was being awarded Prime Minister Fellowship 2022. Since his graduation period, he has won and participated in numerous national and international architectural competition. His interest in architecture took him to numerous national & international workshops. Few of them was with Peter Stuchbury, Anupama Kundoo, Marina Tabassum & Rafiq Azam. His continued engagement with design and making culminated in 2023 at the Design & Make Visiting School, “Maeda Hooke Park,” at the Architectural Association school of architecture, Hooke Park campus. 

His Graduation thesis project "Breathing Hydrological Infrastructure" has been nominated for Archiprix International Hunter Douglas Award 2017. It is the only nomination for Bangladesh since Archiprix started. In 2017, he worked on a publication on the works of an Indian Architect Titled "Anupama Kundoo: Building Knowledge, Building Community"; published from Germany. From May 25, 2018- June 01, 2018 with the collaboration from Archiprix International & BUET he curated an exhibition titled "Archiprix Exhibition Bangladesh 2018". He is now teaching and doing his architectural practice from Chittagong, Bangladesh. His projects has been exhibited in numerous national and international exhibitions in Bangladesh, India and United Kingdom.
