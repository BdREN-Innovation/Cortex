Mr. Ratan Kumar Das is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), currently on study leave while pursuing his PhD in Mechanical, Manufacturing, and Mechatronic Engineering at RMIT University, Melbourne, Australia. He also serves as a Casual Academic at RMIT University and a Casual Lecturer at the Engineering Institute of Technology (EIT), where he teaches across thermal-fluid sciences, computational methods, materials engineering, and engineering design.

His doctoral research focuses on low-temperature eductor-based membrane distillation, combining thermo-fluid sciences, multiphase flow modelling, waste-heat utilization, and advanced CFD techniques to develop sustainable freshwater production systems. His broader research interests include desalination technologies, green hydrogen and fuel-cell systems, waste heat recovery, renewable energy, plasma applications, and numerical simulation. He has published extensively in high-impact journals such as Desalination, Applied Thermal Engineering, International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, Energy Engineering, and Physics of Plasmas. His works also include conference contributions across fluid mechanics, renewable energy, thermal systems, aerodynamics, and micro-scale discharge physics.

With more than ten years of university teaching and research experience, he has taught a broad range of courses including Fluid Mechanics, Advanced Fluid Dynamics, Applied Thermodynamics, Thermal-Fluid System Design, Mechanics of Solids, Vibration and Machine Design, Refrigeration and Air Conditioning, Engineering Materials, MATLAB, and more. He has supervised numerous postgraduate and undergraduate theses, covering CFD-based aerodynamic analyses, fatigue and failure studies, heat transfer enhancement, nanofluid applications, renewable energy devices, and mechanical design optimisation.

In addition to academic teaching and supervision, he has contributed to professional consultancy work through BRTC (CUET), served in several adjunct faculty roles in national institutions, and has been involved in organising major international conferences in mechanical and renewable energy engineering. He is an active reviewer for leading journals including Desalination, Physics of Plasmas, and the Journal of Mechanical Science and Technology, and is a member of professional bodies such as ASME, BSME, the Institution of Engineers Bangladesh, AF-TES, and the Australasian Association for Engineering Education.

His academic achievements include prestigious scholarships and research training awards such as the RMIT Research Stipend Scholarship (RRSS), international graduate scholarships from the University of Ulsan, and multiple grants from Memorial University of Newfoundland. He also holds the distinction of securing 1st place in his BSc Mechanical Engineering cohort at CUET.
