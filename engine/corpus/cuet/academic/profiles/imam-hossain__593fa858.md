**Imam Hossain Chowdhury** is a Lecturer at the Institute of Earthquake Engineering Research (IEER), Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). He previously served as a Lecturer in the Department of Civil Engineering at the Bangladesh Military Academy (BMA), an extended campus of the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), where he gained experience in undergraduate engineering education.

He obtained his B.Sc. in Civil Engineering from CUET and is currently pursuing an M.Sc. in Earthquake Engineering. His academic and research interests include structural dynamics, earthquake engineering, finite element modeling, composite structures, seismic base isolation, and structural fire protection, with a focus on developing sustainable and resilient structural systems.

Mr. Chowdhury has authored and co-authored several peer-reviewed conference publications, including works published in Springer book series, and has presented his research at international conferences. His technical expertise includes ANSYS, ETABS, CSiBridge, AutoCAD, Python, and C++, with applied experience in finite element analysis, structural analysis and design, bridge and transportation engineering, and water supply and sanitation systems.

In addition to his academic responsibilities, he is actively involved in professional development, technical competitions, and academic service, having served as a competition coordinator, content writer, judge, and event organizer at national-level engineering events. His professional interests focus on research-driven teaching, interdisciplinary collaboration, and the development of resilient and sustainable infrastructure.
