I am Syeda Tahmina Tasnim, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Architecture at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). My academic journey began with achieving first-class honors and ranking first in my Bachelor of Architecture program at CUET, and has since evolved into a passion for uncovering and preserving the rich architectural heritage of Bangladesh and South Asia.

As an educator, I have taught diverse courses ranging from design studios and architectural graphics to heritage conservation and building technology, always striving to inspire the next generation of architects to think critically about our built environment. My research focuses on architectural history, theory, and heritage preservation, with particular interest in colonial-indigenous architectural synthesis and community-centered conservation approaches.

Through my publications and ongoing research, I am committed to decolonizing architectural history and contributing to a more inclusive understanding of South Asian architectural heritage. I believe that architecture is not just about buildings—it's about the stories, cultures, and communities that shape our spaces.

I hold a Bachelor of Architecture degree from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), where I graduated first in my class with first-class honors. I completed my Master of Architecture at Bangladesh University of Engineering & Technology (BUET) in History, Theory and Criticism stream.
