Monowar Wadud Hridoy is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechatronics and Industrial Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Chittagong, Bangladesh. He began his academic career on 10 January 2019 and has since demonstrated a strong commitment to teaching, research, and academic service. He obtained his Bachelor of Science (BSc) degree in Mechanical Engineering in 2018 and his Master of Science (MSc) degree in Mechanical Engineering in 2022, both from CUET.

In addition to his teaching responsibilities, Mr. Hridoy has undertaken several significant academic and administrative roles. He served as Assistant Provost of Dr. Qudrat-E-Khuda Hall at CUET and as Treasurer of the CUET Teachers’ Association Executive Committee for the 2024–2025 term. He has also contributed as a Faculty Advisor to various student organizations, including the RMA, Supply Chain and Business Alliance, and the IEOM Student Chapter at CUET, among others.

Moreover, Mr. Hridoy was a member of the Organizing Committee of the 6th IEOM Bangladesh Conference held in 2023 and served as a Co-Chair of a technical session, contributing to the academic quality and successful execution of the conference.

Currently, he is pursuing his Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) degree in Japan as a recipient of the prestigious Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship, where he continues to advance his research and academic career.
