Prof. Dr. Abu Shadat Muhammad Sayem working as a Head of the Department of Petroleum and Mining Engineering, CUET. Under his leadership PME CUET consecutively listed 4 times in QS World ranking (155th position in 2026). 

In addition he is a Professor of Mechanical Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. Prof. Dr. Sayem also working as an Adjunct Senior Research Fellow, Mechanical Discipline, CQ University, Australia. Prior to that Dr. Sayem working as a Mechanical Design Engineer at HDE engineering, A reputed Mining Company at Emerald, Australia.

Dr. Sayem has an appreciable blend of academic background and work experience with University, Industry both in Australia, Japan and Bangladesh in the area of STEM and Clean Energy and sustainability sciences, Energy conversion systems, along with Industrial design and consultancy services approximate around 20 years. Dr. Sayemhas been involved in teaching and research at HED since 2005 just after graduated from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh.

He received his Master of Mechanical Engineering degree from Hokkaido University, Japan and has been awarded with Ph.D. from CQ University, Australia. In addition, Dr. Sayem completed a post graduate diploma on Sustainability Sciences from Hokkaido University and University Tokyo, Japan. He is now working as a Professor of Mechanical Engineering, CUET, Bangladesh and also working as a research fellow, CQ University, Rockhampton in funded project from Stanwell Corporation, Australia.He has published 2 Books and 71 Journal and conference articles and author of a book. In addition, Dr Sayem also work as an evaluator of external research grant application. Dr Sayem also working as a visiting researcher at Hokkaido University, Japan in 2024, in the area of clean energy system (Lithium – Air Battery) and also work as an International Visiting Research Fellow at AUT, New Zealand in June 2024. In addition, Prof. Dr. Sayem selected as a Flying Scientist under Wonder of Science 2024-2025 Flying Scientists program, Queensland. Now Dr. Sayem working as a Adjunct senior research fellow at CQU,Australia and Visiting research fellow at QUT, Australia. 

In leadership capacity, Dr. Sayem performs his role as a President of CUET Teachers Associations for the period of 2022-23, Prior to that Dr. Sayem working as a member RHDC, RECAB at CQ University, Australia and Vice Chairman Academic, E3 program, Hokkaido University, Japan. Dr. Sayem has extensive experience about research grant writing and management along with wide range of collaboration at Higher Degree Institution around the world, particularly Europe , Australia, Japan , New Zealand and USA.
