# Nahida Sultana

Designation: Assistant Professor

Department: Humanities

Email: chaityengdu@cuet.ac.bd

Status: running

Nahida Sultana is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Humanities at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. She teaches English language and communication courses to engineering students and provides professional development trainings for engineers and technical professionals. She is the awardee of Fulbright FLTA for the academic year 2023-24 and has recently completed her appointment  at Cornell University, NY, USA. As a Fulbright scholar, Nahida Sultana serves as a cultural ambassador by representing Bangladeshi language, culture, and values while fostering cross-cultural understanding and academic exchange in the United States. She has taught elementary, middle and high school students, adult learners, immigrants, and international students at multiple schools, colleges and universities across Bangladesh and the U.S. She accomplished her Bachelor of Arts in English and Master of Arts with English literature major from the University of Dhaka. Her academic and research interests include multilingualism, minority, equity, STEM education, language policy, curriculum & instruction, etc. Nahida's interdisciplinary research has extended beyond traditional fields into areas such as identity, gender, culture, race, diversity, society, power and justice. She has published several research articles in various national and international journals. Through her teaching experience from elementary to tertiary level and in two different countries as well, she has subjugated the ways to deal with learners of all ages. She is also a story writer, a poet, a poem reciter, an anchor and a socio-cultural activist who puts emphasis on co/extra-curricular activities and institutional education with an equal proportion to ensure effective as well as sustainable learning.
