**Mr. Redoy Masum Meraz **has been working as an Assistant Professor at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) since 17th April 2025. Before joining as an Assistant Professor, he worked as a Lecturer (8th January 2023 - 16th April 2025) and Research Assistant at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, CUET (31 August 2022-31 December 2022).

He completed his M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at CUET. He accomplished his B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from CUET in 2022 and holds 1st position in the department with a CGPA of 3.95.

His research interests are Renewable Energy, Biodiesel, and composite materials. Currently, he is researching biomass gasification to examine the characteristics of synthesized gas and the performance of the engine using syngas as fuel.
