Md. Asifur Rahman is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) since June 30, 2025. His core academic interests and scholarly pursuits are focused on advancing concrete technology to promote sustainability in infrastructural development.

Mr. Rahman completed both of his M. Sc. and B. Sc. in Civil Engg. from Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology. Prior to his appointment as a faculty member at CUET, he served as a Lecturer at Presidency University (PU), Dhaka, from September 5, 2021, to February 21, 2022, where he began his academic career. Subsequently, he moved to the Department of Civil Engineering at Dhaka University of Engineering & Technology (DUET), Gazipur, and also served as a Lecturer there from February 22, 2022, to July 16, 2022.

During his time at DUET, Mr. Rahman actively contributed not only as an academician but also as a key member of the Consultancy Research & Testing Services (CRTS), providing professional services to governmental institutions, non-governmental organizations, and various private enterprises. He continues to apply his expertise in professional services as a member of the Bureau of Research, Testing, and Consultation (BRTC) at CUET.

Presently, Mr. Rahman is the academic course coordinator for the 2022 Batch in the Department of Civil Engineering. In addition to his academic responsibilities, he served as Publication Secretary of the CUET Teachers' Association during the 2024-2025 tenure.
