About Myself

I'm a professor in the Dept. of Computer Science & Engineering, Director of Institute of Information and Communication Technology (IICT) and Director of CUET IT Business Incubator at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). He was head of the Department of Computer Science & Engineering and Dean of the Faculty of Electrical & Computer Engineering, CUET. Dr. Hoque, with a Ph. D in Information & Computer Sciences from Saitama University (Japan), M.Sc. Engg. in CSE from BUET and B. Sc. Engg. in EEE from CUET, respectively, is the founding director of CUET Natural Language Processing (NLP) Lab. Currently, Dr. Hoque is acting as the Chair of the IEEE Bangladesh Section. He also worked as the head of the self-assessment committee (Department of CSE, CUET) and as the sub-project manager to establish a Fab lab in CUET funded by the World Bank under HEQEP, UGC. Mr. Hoque is working as an Expert Member of the evaluation committee at the Board of Accreditation of Engineering & Technology Education (BAETE), Bangladesh. His research works received several grants from the ICT Division, Bangladesh High Tech Park Authority, University Grants Commission of Bangladesh, Ulster University (UK), Athabasca University (Canada), York University (Canada), and La Trobe University (Australia). Dr. Hoque's academic excellence is not only extensive but also internationally recognized. His publication record boasts over 230 papers in various International Journals and Conferences. His research works have been acknowledged and awarded at several international conferences, including HIS 2011, EICT 2013, IEMIS 2018, ICO 2019, ICIoTCT 2020, CONSTRAINT-AAAI 2021, ICCIT 2022, and IEEE WIECON-ECE 2022. Notably, one of his works received the Champion Award at the Digital Innovation Fair 2016 at the Division & District Level and secured the 2nd prize in a national-level competition in the Solve-a-Thon Category. Dr. Hoque's expertise is further demonstrated through his active role as a reviewer in several reputable journals and conferences, such as IEEE Access, Journal of Soft Computing, IEEE/ACM HRI, IEEE/RSJ IROS, and more. I'm not just a member, but an active participant in several activities of IEEE. His role as an IEEE volunteer and his service in several positions of IEEE Bangladesh Section (BDS) and Technical Society Chapters as an Excom member demonstrate his dedication and commitment. He has served as the IEEE BDS Vice-chair (Technical) for the years 2019 and 2020, Conference Coordinator (2018), Award Coordinator (2017), Award Coordinator & Vice-chair (Activity) of the IEEE CS Bangladesh Chapter, and Educational Activity Coordinator (2017-19) of IEEE RAS Bangladesh Chapter. Moreover, he has served as the IEEE CUET SB, RAS/SPS CUET Chapter, and WIE Affinity Group from 2019-2021 as an advisor. Dr. Hoque worked in several technical committees of IEEE/IEEE BDS co-sponsored conferences such as Organizing Chair (ECCE 2023, ICCIT 2022/23/24, IEEE WEICON-ECE 2022/23/24), TPC Chair (ACMI 2021, ICREST 2021/23/25, ICISET 2024, ECCE 2019, IEEE R10 HTC 2017), TPC Co-chair (IEEE R10 IEEE HTC 17, TENSYMP 2020, ICISET 2018/21, IEEE WIECON-ECE 21) & Publication Chair (IEEE ECE WIECON 2018-19, IEEER10 TENSYMP 2020). His research interests include Human-Robot/Computer Interaction, Machine Learning, and Natural Language Processing. Dr. Hoque is a Senior Member of IEEE, IEEE Computer Society, IEEE Robotics & Automation Society, IEEE Women in Engineering Affinity Group, and IEEE Signal Processing Society, USA. He is also a Fellow of the Institute of Engineers, Bangladesh.
