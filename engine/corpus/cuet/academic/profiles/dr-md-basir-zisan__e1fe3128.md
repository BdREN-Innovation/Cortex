#### **Dissertation: **Assessment of Seismic Protection Effectiveness of Unbonded Scrap Tire Rubber Pad Base Isolation Using Finite Element Analysis.

The assessment of seismic protection effectiveness of unbonded scrap tire rubber pad base isolation using finite element analysis offers a promising and innovative low-cost solution for developing countries. Scrap tires, widely available as waste, can be repurposed into low-cost isolation pads, significantly reducing construction cost while enhancing seismic resilience. Finite element analysis demonstrates that these rubber pads effectively mitigate seismic forces transmitted to structures, thus improving safety and performance. This sustainable approach not only addresses environmental concerns associated with tire disposal but also supports affordable earthquake-resistant construction, making it particularly suitable for resource-limited regions vulnerable to seismic hazards.
, PhD, Civil Engineering (Earthquake Risk Reduction)

#### Dissertation: Dynamic Behavior and Distortion-Induced Stress in Curved Multi I-Girder Bridge Under Moving Vehicles.

This research investigates the dynamic behavior and fatigue issues of composite multi-girder curved bridges, particularly in highway interchanges, using a computationally efficient Bridge Vehicle Interaction (BVI) model. A 3D finite element approach analyzes the effects of bridge curvature, skew angle, and vehicle parameters on distortion-induced fatigue and deformation. Results indicate that vehicle eccentricity and road roughness amplify dynamic responses, especially Dynamic Amplification Factors (DAF) and bearing forces. Highly curved and skewed bridges exhibit greater distortion stresses and differential deflections, contributing to fatigue damage. The study highlights limitations in current practices for predicting such stresses in curved bridge systems.
, M.Sc. Engineering, Civil Engineering (Bridge Engineering)
