Dr. Bipul Chandra Mondal is a dedicated academic and researcher in the field of civil engineering, currently contributing to the Department of Civil Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). His research focuses on pipeline integrity assessment, corrosion effects on structural materials, and sustainable construction practices, with numerous publications in reputable journals such as the *Journal of Pipeline Systems Engineering and Practice* and *Construction and Building Materials*.​

#### Academic and Research Profile

Dr. Mondal holds a Ph.D. and has been actively engaged in research on the limit state of corroded pipelines, fracture mechanics, and the application of industrial byproducts like water treatment sludge in concrete. His work combines experimental and finite element analysis to evaluate burst pressure in pipelines under various defect conditions, contributing significantly to offshore and onshore pipeline safety. He has also investigated environmental impacts on structural concrete and alternative cementitious materials, demonstrating a strong commitment to sustainable infrastructure development.​

#### Professional Contributions

He has been serving in the Department of Civil Engineering at CUET since November 2020 and has previously held academic and research positions since 2005. Dr. Mondal is an active member of professional organizations including the Institution of Engineers, Bangladesh, and the Canadian Geotechnical Society, reflecting his engagement with the global engineering community. His research has been recognized with awards, including the Best Paper Award at the 7th International Conference on Civil Engineering for Sustainable Development in 2024.​
