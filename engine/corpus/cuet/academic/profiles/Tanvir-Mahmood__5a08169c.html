              Hi! I’m Tanvir Mahmood—urban planner, researcher, and changemaker. My focus? Sustainable cities, climate resilience, and the impact of infrastructure on people and places. Currently, I’m exploring how the Karnafuli Tunnel is transforming land use and livelihoods in the Bairag Union.

With a blend of data, design, and real-world context, I aim to drive smarter planning decisions. My work spans publications, field research, and collaboration — always grounded in impact. I’m passionate about turning research into solutions that matter.

Always open to new ideas, global connections, and bold solutions for a more sustainable future. Let’s build resilient, inclusive futures — together!
