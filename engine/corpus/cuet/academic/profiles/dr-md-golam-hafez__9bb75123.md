Dr. Md. Golam Hafez is a Professor in the Department of Mathematics at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. Since 11 October 2025, he has also served as the Provost of Shahid Mohammad Shah Hall at CUET. He holds a Ph.D. in Theoretical Plasma Physics from the University of Rajshahi (2018) and an M.Phil. in Mathematical Physics from the University of Chittagong (2011). Dr. Hafez has received several academic distinctions, including the Shaha Moukdum Hall Gold Medal for outstanding undergraduate performance and the CUET Best Research Award (2019). In addition, he was recognized among the World’s Top 2% Scientists in 2022 and 2023, as listed by Stanford University.

Dr. Hafez has held several international academic positions, including Visiting Scientist at Bharathiar University, Coimbatore, India, under the India Science and Research Fellowship (ISRF), and Visiting Professor at Periyar University, Salem, India. His research focuses on theoretical and computational plasma physics, mathematical physics, fractional calculus, analytical and numerical solutions of nonlinear evolution equations, bifurcation analysis, and chaotic dynamics. He also contributes to interdisciplinary fields such as biomathematics and control engineering.
