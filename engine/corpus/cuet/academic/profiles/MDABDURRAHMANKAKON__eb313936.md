*I am Md Abdur Rahman, an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechatronics and Industrial Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh, currently on study leave pursuing my PhD at Saga University, Japan, under the prestigious Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship.*

*My research sits at the intersection of **Acoustic Emission Tomography, Biomedical Imaging, and Machine Learning** — with a current focus on developing an advanced Knee Osteoarthritis (KOA) visualization system using enhanced source localization techniques. I hold a Master of Engineering in Biomedical Engineering from Saga University (CGPA 3.90/4.00) and a B.Sc. in Electrical & Electronic Engineering from CUET, where I graduated 8th in a class of 135 students.*

*With over six years of teaching and research experience across Bangladesh and Japan, I am passionate about bridging engineering and medicine through innovative signal and image processing solutions. I actively collaborate on international research projects, have presented at conferences across Japan, Korea, and the Netherlands, and am deeply committed to mentoring the next generation of engineers.*

*Beyond research, I embrace cross-cultural learning — having participated in exchange programs, industry visits, and leadership initiatives across Japan — and remain driven by a curiosity for emerging technologies and their real-world applications.*
