Protick Chandra Ghosh is a Lecturer in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. He was born in Dhaka in 1999 and has been serving at CUET since May 4, 2025.

Protick completed his B.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering from CUET in June 2024 and is currently pursuing his M.Sc. in the same field at the same institution. His academic journey reflects a deep commitment to learning and teaching, and he is passionate about contributing to both the educational and personal development of his students.

He looks forward to continuous growth — both as an educator and a lifelong learner.
