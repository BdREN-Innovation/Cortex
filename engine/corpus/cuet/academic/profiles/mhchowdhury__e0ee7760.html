I completed my SSC from Ideal School & College, Motijheel, Dhaka, and my HSC from Dhaka College, Dhaka. I received my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), and my Ph.D. in Electrical Engineering from City University of Hong Kong as an HKPFS fellow. After graduation, I worked as a Software Engineer at Samsung R&D Institute Bangladesh Ltd., and in 2013,  I joined the Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at CUET, where I am currently serving as a Professor.

My research lies at the intersection of Signal Processing, Artificial Intelligence, Biomedical Systems, and Hardware-Software Co-design, with a focus on point-of-care health technologies. I am the Founder-Director of the Advanced Electronic Applications Research Lab (AEAR Lab) at CUET and have secured competitive research funding as Principal Investigator for multiple projects. My work has led to 20+ publications in leading journals and 2 granted U.S. patents. Alongside teaching and research, I remain actively involved in academic and professional service as Advisor of the CUET Debating Society and CUET Career Club, Counselor of the IEEE CUET Student Branch, Executive Member of IEEE R10 SAC, and Secretary of BRTC-EEE at CUET.
