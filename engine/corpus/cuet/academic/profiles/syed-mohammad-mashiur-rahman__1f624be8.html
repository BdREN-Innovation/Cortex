🎓 currently serves as an Assistant Professor of English in the Department of Humanities at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET). 

While he has been fortunate to earn his Bachelor's and Master's degrees in English from Jahangirnagar University with distinction, he has come to realize that academic credentials mark just the beginning of one's learning journey.

Every day in the classroom, as he teaches, he finds himself learning just as much. His research in sociolinguistics and professional communication remains a work in progress. With each paper he reads and each study he conducts, he becomes more aware of how much more there is to explore. He has always believed that language is among humanity's most profound gifts—it has the power to shape worlds, bridge divides, and touch souls. A single word, in his view, can spark revolutions or bring peace; a well-crafted sentence can bring tears to one's eyes or light up a face with joy. This remarkable duality is what drew him to study and now teach it. The most rewarding part of his work comes from witnessing his students grow.

If there is one lesson he hopes to impart, it is that education isn't about reaching some final destination of knowledge. It's about the humility to keep learning, the curiosity to keep questioning, and the courage to keep evolving—principles he strives to live by every day in both his teaching and his own ongoing education.

Syed Mohammad Mashiur Rahman: Role in Engineering Education at CUET

Syed Mohammad Mashiur Rahman's current tenure as a faculty of English in the Department of Humanities at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET) is Active. His courses—Technical English, Theoretical English, Language Development, Fundamental English, and Communication Skills—are integral components of CUET's undergraduate engineering curriculum. These courses develop essential communication and critical thinking skills required for professional success in engineering fields.

🔍Course Objectives and Outcomes

💡Technical English

Focuses on technical writing, report preparation, and documentation

Prepares students to communicate complex engineering concepts clearly

💡Theoretical English

Enhances analytical reading and argumentation skills

Develops capacity for critical evaluation of texts

💡Language Development

Improves grammatical accuracy and fluency in English

Strengthens proficiency for academic and professional contexts

💡Fundamental English

Builds core competencies in reading, writing, and comprehension

Establishes foundation for advanced communication requirements

💡Communication Skills

Develops presentation, negotiation, and interpersonal communication abilities

Prepares students for collaborative work environments

🏆Institutional Context

CUET graduates enter various engineering sectors, including petroleum, mechatronics, civil, electrical, and urban planning. Effective communication skills complement their technical training, enabling them to:

Present research findings

Write technical reports

Engage in professional discourse

Collaborate in multinational environments

While CUET's primary focus remains technical education, language and communication courses ensure graduates meet the comprehensive demands of global engineering careers. These skills contribute to professional advancement, though technical expertise remains the primary determinant of career progression.

📌To Summarize:

Mashiur Rahman's instruction in English and communication supports CUET's mission to produce well-rounded engineers. His courses address the linguistic and rhetorical requirements of engineering professions, providing students with necessary tools for academic and career development.
