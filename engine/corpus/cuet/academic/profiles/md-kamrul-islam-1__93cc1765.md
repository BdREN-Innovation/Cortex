Md. Kamrul Islam is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Urban and Regional Planning at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. He holds a Master’s degree in Urban and Regional Planning from CUET, where his research focused on *vulnerability to coastal erosion and community adaptation strategies*. He completed his Bachelor’s degree from the same institution with outstanding academic performance , concentrating on *spatio-temporal ecological disturbances in cyclone-prone coastal Areas*.

With a strong academic and professional background in **urban planning, disaster risk management, and geospatial technologies**, he has contributed significantly to both teaching and applied research. His research interests encompass **disaster management, climate change adaptation, ecosystem services, loss and damage, spatial modeling and simulation, and the integration of GIS and remote sensing in urban and environmental studies**.

He has authored and co-authored numerous publications in internationally recognized journals, addressing issues such as **coastal erosion, shoreline change, urban heat islands, wetland dynamics, flood vulnerability, and ecological resilience**. Beyond academic publications, he has presented his work at various international conferences, receiving recognition, including the **Best Research Paper Award (2024)** at the 7th International Conference on Advances in Civil Engineering, CUET.

Md. Kamrul Islam has also been actively involved in several funded research projects supported by national and international organizations, such as the **UK Research and Innovation Collective Fund, Asian Disaster Preparedness Centre, Bill & Melinda Gates Foundation, and UCL Consultants Ltd.** These projects highlight his expertise in disaster risk reduction, climate resilience, and community-based adaptation strategies.

In addition to research, he has served as a **trainer** in professional courses on GIS, remote sensing, and smart mapping applications, contributing to capacity-building initiatives for government officials, professionals, and students. His technical skills include proficiency in **ArcGIS, ERDAS Imagine, ILWIS, Google Earth Engine, AutoCAD, SPSS, and R**, making him well-equipped for advanced geospatial and quantitative analysis.

Through his teaching, research, and professional collaborations, Md. Kamrul Islam continues to work towards building climate-resilient and sustainable urban and regional systems in Bangladesh and beyond.
