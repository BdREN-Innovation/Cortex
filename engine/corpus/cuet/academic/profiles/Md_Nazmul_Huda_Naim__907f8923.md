Md. Nazmul Huda Naim is currently serving as an Assistant Professor in the Department of Urban and Regional Planning at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). He obtained his Bachelor of Urban and Regional Planning (BURP) degree with outstanding academic performance and was awarded the Prime Minister’s Gold Medal 2018 by the University Grants Commission of Bangladesh in 2020. Throughout his academic career, he has received several scholarships and academic distinctions in recognition of his achievements.

Prior to joining academia, Mr. Naim gained professional experience in the development sector. He worked as an Information Management and Reporting Officer (IMRO) at BRAC in 2020 and later served as Camp IM Manager at NGO Forum for Public Health, where he was involved in managing and analyzing WASH relevant data to support humanitarian and development interventions.

His academic interests have evolved from transportation planning at the undergraduate level to disaster management and environmental issues at the postgraduate level. He is currently pursuing a Master of Urban and Regional Planning (MURP) in CUET, while actively engaging in teaching, research, and academic supervision. His work emphasizes the application of Geographic Information Systems (GIS), Remote Sensing (RS), spatial analysis, and quantitative methods to address planning, environmental, and disaster-related challenges.

At CUET, he is committed to advancing research and education that contribute to sustainable development, climate resilience, and evidence-based planning practices.
