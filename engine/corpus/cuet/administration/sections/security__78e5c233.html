Message from the Head

The Security Section of Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) is dedicated to maintaining a safe, secure, and orderly campus for students, faculty, staff, and visitors. Operating 24/7, the section is staffed by a team of trained security personnel, including supervisors and guards, who work in shifts to ensure continuous coverage.

Key responsibilities include campus surveillance, access control, emergency response, traffic and crowd management, and protection of university assets. Through vigilance, discipline, and coordinated efforts, the Security Section supports a peaceful and secure environment for learning, research, and innovation.
