Message from the Head

Comptroller Office

The Comptroller Office is one of the key points in any University. In a single word, the function of this office is like the blood of the body. At Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), the Office of the Comptroller maintains all kinds of financial activities of the University. It is located on the ground floor of the new administrative building (Administrative Building-2) of CUET.

The Comptroller Office of CUET consists of the following sections:

Salaries, Allowance & Record Section

This section prepares the salaries of teachers, officers, and staff of the university. All types of TA/DA, and general provident funds are also prepared and maintained by this section.

Pension, Welfare fund & Cash Section

Fund: Pension fund, benevolent fund, and all other funds are maintained by this section. This section also takes necessary actions to invest the idle funds of the University.

Cash: All types of contractor bills, utility bills, advance bills, and many other bills are checked and prepared by this section. Disburses and records all types of payments by issuing cheques. The Cash section also maintains petty cash, prepares bank reconciliations, and updates various accounts.

Welfare: Various welfare funds are maintained by this section. This section also carries out all welfare related works of the University.

Budget, Finance, Compilation & Student Affairs Section

 Budget: This section prepares the yearly budget and submits it to the UGC to receive the annual grant. All types of accounts, reporting, receipts, and payments are prepared and maintained by the Finance section. It also takes necessary action to submit any kind of report to the UGC upon request.

Student Affairs:  Student affairs related all works done by this section. Such as Student scholarships, stipends, student collections, and other student-related activities.

Dispatch Section

All incoming letters, bills, and files are received and distributed to the relevant sections by this section. At the same time, all outgoing letters and files are dispatched by this section.

Publications

Prepared yearly budget and submit that’s to UGC to get the annual grant. All types of accounts, reporting, receipts & payments are prepared and maintained by finance section. It also takes necessary action to submit any kinds of report to UGC by asking the commission.
