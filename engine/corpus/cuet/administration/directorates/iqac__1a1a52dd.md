## Message from the Head

Greetings from the Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) of Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), Bangladesh. The Institutional Quality Assurance Cell (IQAC) has been established to develop a mechanism for systematic review of academic programs and entities, ensure quality teaching-learning, research, knowledge generation and support service standards at an acceptable level. Understanding the emerging need for quality in tertiary education, the IQAC was established at CUET in 2014. Since its inception, IQAC has been committed to innovation in learning, teaching and research activities at the university.

IQAC continuously tries to improve the teaching- learning environment and update the curricula through academic rigor at the university. It is warmly assisting the quality assurance activities at CUET. The IQAC promises to higher education stakeholders and the region particularly to ensure its accountability and transparency in the quality decision making and management system of the institution. It is responsible for ensuring that the quality assurance policies and procedures of the university are in place and in practice. We seek your precious support and sincere cooperation in this journey of IQAC and we strongly believe that, with your support, IQAC at CUET can be a forerunner in the higher education domain of Bangladesh. We hope that your support and kind cooperation will always encourage us in future.
