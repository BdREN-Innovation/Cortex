Message from the Head

Welcome to Directorate of Research Extension (DRE)

Welcome to the Directorate of Research and Extension (DRE) at Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET)!

I am delighted to extend my warmest greetings to all the postgraduate students, teachers, researchers, and visitors who have found their way to our website. As the Director of DRE, it is my privilege to lead this dynamic and dedicated team that is committed to fostering a culture of research excellence and providing comprehensive support to our academic community.

At DRE, our primary focus is to facilitate and enhance research and extension activities within CUET. We understand the vital role that research plays in advancing knowledge, driving innovation, and addressing societal challenges. Therefore, we strive to create an environment that encourages and nurtures research endeavors across various disciplines.

One of our key responsibilities is to organize and oversee the Committee for Higher Studies and Research (CHSR) Meetings and Research Evaluation Committee (REC) Meetings. These platforms provide a valuable opportunity for scholars and researchers to present their work, receive constructive feedback, and collaborate with peers. Through these meetings, we aim to promote interdisciplinary research, foster intellectual exchange, and contribute to the academic growth of our institution.

Furthermore, we are dedicated to supporting postgraduate students, teachers, and researchers in their pursuit of knowledge and excellence. Whether you are seeking guidance on research methodologies, funding opportunities, or publication strategies, our team is here to assist you every step of the way. We believe in empowering our academic community with the necessary resources and mentorship to thrive in their research endeavors.

I encourage you to explore our website and discover the wide range of services and resources that DRE offers. From research grants and scholarships to workshops and seminars, we strive to provide a comprehensive ecosystem that nurtures research and innovation. Our goal is to create an inclusive and collaborative environment where ideas flourish and transformative research emerges.

I extend my gratitude to the entire DRE team for their unwavering dedication and commitment to advancing research and extension activities at CUET. Together, we will continue to foster a culture of intellectual curiosity, innovation, and academic excellence.

Thank you for visiting our website, and I invite you to join us on this exciting journey of knowledge creation and dissemination.
