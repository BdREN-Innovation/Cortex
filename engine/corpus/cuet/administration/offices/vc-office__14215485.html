Message from the Head

The Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) embarked on its historic journey with a profound mission: to develop globally competitive human resources in engineering, technology, and innovation for the service of the nation and humanity. Over the past 57 years, since its inception as an engineering college in 1968, CUET has continued to expand its excellence, uphold academic distinction, and strengthen its legacy as a center of advanced engineering education in Bangladesh.

Today, CUET comprises 18 academic departments and 4 dedicated research institutes, offering a comprehensive spectrum of modern engineering disciplines. Each year, around 931 brilliant undergraduate students are admitted through one of the most rigorous and competitive admission tests in the country into 12 degree-awarding departments. Our vibrant postgraduate programs—including M.Sc., M.Sc. Engg., M.Engg., M.Phil., and Ph.D.—operate across 11 departments, engaging emerging scholars in high-impact research and innovation.

To fulfill our core mission, we attract the brightest minds and continuously upgrade our curricula to match global standards and industrial relevance. CUET’s strategic geographic location as the only engineering university in the south-eastern region of Bangladesh provides our students unprecedented opportunities for hands-on industrial exposure. Chattogram, home to the country’s premier seaport and a thriving cluster of heavy and light industries, acts as a natural learning ecosystem that enriches our students’ technical and professional competencies.

At the same time, CUET remains steadfast in fostering international collaborations with leading global universities and research institutions. Recognizing Bangladesh’s unique geographical vulnerabilities as a deltaic and disaster-prone nation, we have initiated specialized postgraduate and research programs in Disaster Engineering and Management, as well as established the Earthquake Engineering Research Institute and the River, Harbor & Environmental Science Institute. These initiatives reflect our unwavering commitment to conducting research that is socially relevant, globally significant, and nationally transformative.

Furthermore, CUET is strongly promoting industry–academia partnerships to drive innovation, entrepreneurship, and technological leadership. We take immense pride in our alumni, who are now excelling in prestigious national and international roles, contributing to academia, industry, public service, and global technological advancement.

Looking ahead, CUET has formulated a bold and strategic development roadmap to establish 21 departments, 4 institutes, 9 directorates, and a state-of-the-art Central Research Laboratory by 2032. This ambitious expansion reflects our vision of transforming CUET into a leading hub of engineering research, innovation, and higher education in the region.

As Vice-Chancellor, I extend a sincere invitation to our alumni, development partners, industries, donor organizations, and philanthropists to join hands with us in realizing CUET’s full potential. Your support and collaboration will greatly contribute to the socio-economic development of Bangladesh and the advancement of global knowledge and technological progress.

Finally, it is my pleasure to warmly welcome you to our naturally serene and intellectually vibrant CUET campus, where innovation thrives, and the future is being shaped each day.

 

Dr. Abu Shadat Muhammad Sayem

Vice-Chancellor

Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET)
