Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) proudly marks its 50th anniversary as a leading engineering institution in Bangladesh. Originating as the Bangladesh Institute of Technology (BIT) in 1973 and transitioning to CUET in 2003, the university has spent half a century pioneering engineering education and research. CUET's golden jubilee celebrations will span the entire year, featuring academic seminars, cultural programs, alumni reunions, and special events showcasing its remarkable journey and vision for the future. These events aim to highlight CUET’s pivotal role in shaping the nation's engineering landscape and its continued commitment to fostering innovation and academic excellence. Over the past 50 years, CUET has solidified its reputation as a hub of cutting-edge research, producing skilled engineers and visionaries who have made significant contributions to national and international industries. The institution's legacy reflects a blend of tradition and forward-thinking, as it continues to inspire future generations.As CUET celebrates this milestone, it reaffirms its dedication to driving progress, promoting sustainable development, and maintaining its position as a beacon of engineering education in Bangladesh and beyond.
