🎓 MSc Thesis Defence Successfully Completed

The Department of Chemistry, Chittagong University of Engineering and Technology (CUET), is pleased to share that the MSc thesis defence of Meherunnesa Prima, Student ID: 22MSCHEM110, Session 2022–2023, has been successfully completed.

Thesis Title:
“PREDICTIVE MODELING AND OPTIMIZATION OF CO₂ PHOTOCONVERSION WITH TiO₂-BASED CATALYSTS USING MACHINE LEARNING AND METAHEURISTICS”

The Department extends its sincere gratitude to the respected External Member, Professor Dr. Mohammad Ismail, Former Vice-Chancellor, Noakhali Science and Technology University, and Professor, Department of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering (ACCE), University of Dhaka, Dhaka, Bangladesh, for his valuable time, insightful evaluation, constructive comments, and kind presence at the thesis defence.

We also express our heartfelt thanks to the Supervisor, Co-supervisor, faculty members, examiners, and all other distinguished members who were present at the defence and contributed their valuable time, comments, and suggestions to make the academic event meaningful and successful.

The Department congratulates Meherunnesa Prima on the successful completion of her thesis defence and wishes her every success in her future academic and research career. 🎉
