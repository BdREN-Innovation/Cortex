The **CUET Model United Nations 2025** is an exciting platform for aspiring leaders and changemakers to engage in global diplomacy. Hosted by Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET), this prestigious event brings together bright minds from around the world to debate pressing international issues, develop policy solutions, and enhance skills in negotiation and public speaking. With diverse committees, dynamic discussions, and a spirit of collaboration, CUET MUN 2025 aims to inspire participants to think critically and act decisively for a better future. Join us to experience the power of youth-driven diplomacy and global impact!
