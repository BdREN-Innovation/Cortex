The Institute of Information and Communication Technology (IICT) proudly hosted a seminar titled "Shaping the Future with Technology" on January 12, 2025. The event was attended by students, faculty members, and technology enthusiasts from various institutions. Renowned speakers from the tech industry delivered insightful sessions on the latest trends in Artificial Intelligence, the practical applications of machine learning, and the importance of robust data security frameworks. The keynote speaker, Dr. Anika Rahman, emphasized the transformative potential of AI in revolutionizing industries like healthcare and education. A highlight of the seminar was the interactive panel discussion featuring experts from leading tech companies. They engaged with the audience, addressing queries and sharing their professional experiences. The event concluded with a hands-on workshop on data encryption techniques, allowing participants to gain practical knowledge and skills. Speaking on the occasion, the IICT department head, Dr. Mahmudul Hasan, stated, “Our goal is to prepare students to thrive in the digital age. Events like these bridge the gap between academia and industry.” The seminar received overwhelmingly positive feedback from attendees, many of whom expressed interest in similar future events.
