The Festive Annual Sports Competition 2024 concluded with great enthusiasm and fanfare at the Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). The week-long event, held from May 10 to May 17, brought together students, faculty, and staff in a celebration of athletic prowess, teamwork, and university spirit.

This year's competition featured a diverse array of sports, including traditional track and field events, football, basketball, cricket, and volleyball, along with indoor games such as table tennis, chess, and badminton. The opening ceremony was marked by a vibrant parade, where participants from various departments showcased their team spirit with colorful banners and creative costumes.

Highlights of the competition included thrilling matches and record-breaking performances. The football final, which saw the Civil Engineering department team clinch victory in a nail-biting penalty shootout, was a particular crowd favorite. Similarly, the basketball tournament was fiercely contested, with the Mechanical Engineering team emerging as champions after a series of closely fought games.

The event also emphasized inclusivity and participation, featuring friendly matches and fun activities such as tug-of-war and sack races, ensuring that everyone, regardless of their athletic ability, could join in the festivities.

The closing ceremony was a grand affair, attended by dignitaries from CUET and the wider community. Medals and trophies were awarded to the winners by the Vice-Chancellor, who praised the participants for their dedication and sportsmanship. The ceremony concluded with a dazzling fireworks display, leaving attendees with fond memories and a sense of camaraderie.

Overall, the Festive Annual Sports Competition 2024 at CUET was a resounding success, fostering a sense of unity and showcasing the talent and spirit of the university community.
