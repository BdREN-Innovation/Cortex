## Postgraduate Programs in Civil Engineering

The **Department of Civil Engineering at CUET** offers comprehensive **postgraduate programs** designed to provide advanced knowledge and specialization across various fields of civil engineering. These programs are intended for individuals seeking to deepen their understanding of engineering principles and engage in high-level research.

Our **Master’s and Ph.D. programs** prepare students to tackle complex engineering challenges through innovative solutions and research-driven approaches. The programs emphasize both academic rigor and practical application, fostering an environment of intellectual growth, professional development, and research excellence.

### Key Features of the Postgraduate Program

1. **Advanced Research**
Opportunities for in-depth research in specialized areas, including Structural Engineering, Environmental Engineering, Geotechnical Engineering, Transportation Engineering, and Hydraulic Engineering.
1. **Experienced Faculty & Research Facilities**
Access to highly qualified faculty and **state-of-the-art laboratories** for experiments, simulations, and data analysis.
1. **Collaborative Research**
Partnerships with industry, government agencies, and academic institutions enable collaborative projects and real-world applications.
1. **International Exposure**
Participation in global workshops, conferences, and exchange programs provides exposure to international best practices and advancements in civil engineering.
1. **Thesis/Dissertation Focus**
Emphasis on independent research guided by experienced supervisors, addressing contemporary challenges in infrastructure, construction materials, and environmental sustainability.

Whether pursuing an undergraduate degree or a postgraduate specialization, the **Civil Engineering Department at CUET** provides a robust academic environment supported by cutting-edge technology and a dedicated faculty team. Graduates are well-prepared to become **competent, innovative professionals** contributing to the advancement of infrastructure and sustainable development.

## Postgraduate Study Programs

The postgraduate program has a vigorous, rapidly expanding program of advanced study and research in the areas of Power system planning, Energy conversion, Optical fiber communication, Digital signal processing, Power semiconductor circuits, Industrial drives, MOS devices, Microwave theory and techniques, Laser theory, Artificial intelligence, and neural networks, etc. These postgraduate programs lead to the degree of Master of Science in Electrical and Electronic Engineering and Doctor of Philosophy in Electrical and Electronic Engineering.

To be awarded an M. Sc. degree, a student must complete 36 credit hours, of which a maximum of 09 credit hours are for project work or 18 credit hours are for thesis work. The degree requirement for a Ph.D. is 60 credit hours, of which a maximum of 45 credit hours are for the thesis.

### Research Facilities

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering has many advanced instruments and equipment with laboratories for studying various Electrical and Electronic Engineering problems.

#### Research Laboratory: Advanced Power System Laboratory

Objectives:

1. How to design and perform highly detailed simulation of power systems, and emulate the behavior of power system devices in different time scales.
1. How to conduct research and educational activities in the areas of Smart Grids with the changing environment resulting from the dramatic increase in deployments of renewable and distributed energy.
1. How to carry on distributed control and information exchange in the power system elements using compatible communication protocols (e.g., IEC61850).

#### Renewable Energy Lab: Solar Energy - Thin Film Solar Cells

Solar cells are a renewable way of producing electricity from sunlight without wearing out or consuming anything. Thin-film solar cells are the new generation of solar cells containing multiple thin-film layers of photovoltaic materials. The thin-film layers are deposited on a glass or plastic substrate, offering promising options for reducing photovoltaic system costs and increasing solar electricity market penetration.

## Postgraduate Study Program

The Department of Computer Science & Engineering offers two degree programs at the masters level- M.Sc. Engg. and M. Engg. For being qualified to be awarded M.Sc. Engg. degree a student must earn a minimum of 36 credit hours including a thesis of 18 credit hours whereas for M. Engg. degree a student must earn a minimum of 36 credit hours including a thesis of 6 credit hours. The graduate degrees offered in this department provide advanced course works and emphasize student research as well as numerous opportunities for interdisciplinary study. The graduate-level thesis works emphasize research topics on current interest, such as Bioinformatics, Computer Vision, Multimedia Security; Sensor and Ad-hoc Networks, Communication, Wireless Systems and so on. CSE department also offers PhD degree from 2015. For being qualified to be awarded PhD degree a student must earn a minimum of 54 credit hours including a thesis of 45 credit hours.
