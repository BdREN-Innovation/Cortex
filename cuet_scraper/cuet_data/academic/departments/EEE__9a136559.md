## About

Technology has been advancing very fast in the last few decades. Scientists and professionals are concentrating more on automation and communication, which in turn increase the demand for engineers all over the world. So, it is obvious that students have their dreams to be professional in this wonderful sector. We here in EEE department of CUET are wholehearted for these students who would like to see their dreams come to reality.

The department of EEE has been playing a vital role in producing engineers since its inception in 1968. The department runs undergraduate as well as postgraduate to cater to the ever challenging needs of technical excellence in all areas of electrical & electronics engineering. It has distinguished faculty members who are competent and skilled in different fields of electrical & electronics engineering. Our vision is to provide the best opportunities to the students of our beloved country for achieving world-class education to enable them to participate in the development of society.

### **Power Electronics Laboratory**

**What is Power Electronics?**
Power Electronics is a technology to convert one or more properties of electric power, such as voltage, current, frequency, phase angle, number of phases, and waveforms, by using power semiconductor devices and/or circuits. It is a unified research area combining power engineering (electric machines, electric power systems), electronics engineering (semiconductor devices, circuits), and control engineering fields.

Currently, Power Electronics is widely applied in electric power systems such as power generation and transmission systems, industrial systems such as electric motors, fans, pumps, blowers, power supplies for information and communication systems, factory automation, transportation systems such as electric trains, pure and hybrid electric vehicles, home appliances, and more.
