## About

Welcome to the Department of Mathematics at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET). We started our journey in the year of 1968. Over the last five decades, we have grown our expertise and competence in the engineering-related curriculum and core mathematics research. The subject of Mathematics has numerous contributions to designing the Mathematical Model of modern Engineering problems. With the help of basic Mathematical knowledge, students are able to understand the logical system in their application sectors to tackle the real world problems. The primary focus of our curriculum is to impart mathematical technical know-how to students, promote their problem solving skills and innovation of new technologies.
    We have a strong postgraduate level, we offer MPhil and Ph.D. in Mathematics by research. The main goal of this program is to produce quality researchers in Mathematics. The sanctioned students’ strengths of MPhil and Ph.D. by research are 10 and 5 respectively each year. However, it varies with the quality candidates. At present, the department has 08 Ph.D. and 20 MPhil scholars. Students are mostly faculty members from different colleges and universities carry out their postgraduate studies. Besides this, our department managed several undergraduate courses in the different engineering departments at CUET.
  The postgraduate course contents are periodically updated for introducing new Mathematical concepts and logical developments. Postgraduate students are encouraged to undertake various research projects. Our department maintains active research groups for carrying out collaborative and interdisciplinary research. We have state of the art research facilities to support our academic programs and research. Funding is available for research from government and universities' internal sources for students and faculties.
    The Department of Mathematics established a sophisticated Simulation Lab supported by the World Bank through the University Grand Commission (UGC) under the project HEQEP (Higher Education Quality Enhancement Project). The Simulation Lab unveils new opportunities for graduate students to carry on their research with modern computational software in Mathematical Modeling and Simulation.   Our departments’ faculty members are always careful about teaching and research. Faculty members have excellent academic credentials and are highly regarded. They have been conferred with many prestigious awards at national and international levels. Several faculty members serve on the editorial boards of national and international journals, review technical articles for journals on a regular basis, and organize international symposia and conferences.

This department homepage provides an overview of the academic programs, research activities of our department, research facilities, profiles of faculty members, and details of student activities. If you have further questions after browsing this page, please do not hesitate to contact us. The contact details are given below. You may also correspond with individual faculty members, or contact them by email, using the addresses shown on the faculty pages as your need. Our department looks forward to contributing to solving mathematical problems related to the technological challenges of society with active participation from all sections of society. Thank you for visiting our departmental page.

## Vision

The vision of our department is to provide quality education and research in Pure and Applied Mathematics which help us to establish as a center of excellence at the national and international level.

## Mission

As an independent Department and a part of an Engineering University, the Department of Mathematics has following mission: ---To provide quality education primarily in the undergraduate level in different engineering branches as they are able to effectively apply their mathematical logical cognition to solve real-world problems. --- To conduct high quality research at the postgraduate level with an aim to advance knowledge of Pure and applied Mathematics as they become assets of our country. --- To develop new ideas in research and create collaboration with other departments and universities at home and abroad.

## Message from the Head

On behalf of all faculties, staffs and students of the department of Mathematics, I would like to welcome you all to our Department. Everyone knows a little bit of math. Minimum mathematical knowledge is essential for managing life properly. Those who are deprived of education also know how to count. No man can survive without mathematics. Not only human beings but all the creatures of the world have the minimum knowledge of mathematics. Everything from microscopic bacteria to plants, the sky, the air, the planets and stars have a touch of mathematics. It is not possible to keep anything running without mathematical rules. Human civilization has improved with the advancement of mathematics. Many of us do not know this. What we do not know is that behind all that is aesthetically pleasing in our environment lies the proper application of mathematical reasoning. But in childhood, mathematics is presented to us as a scary subject. And its effect lasts a lifetime. Even Einstein said himself “Do not worry too much about your difficulties in mathematics, I can assure you that mine are still greater.”
  The Department of Mathematics at Chittagong University of Engineering & Technology (CUET) was launched with the aim of preparing students and researchers in Mathematics with a width of knowledge of the other sciences and technologies. Besides the offered MPhil and PhD research areas in Mathematics Department, we are responsible for the undergraduate mathematics courses of different engineering branches at CUET.

We have plan to organize an International Conference in Dec’2021 to provide a platform to the researcher in national and international arena to present their research.
